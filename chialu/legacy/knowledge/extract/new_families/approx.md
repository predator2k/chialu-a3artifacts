# new-family review: approx

Source: `run/extract/reduce/newfam_approx.md` (13 proposals). Vocabulary checked
against `chialu/spaces/approx_spaces.py` (`approx_adder_space`, `approx_mul_space`,
`approx_div_space`, `approx_method_space`), the neighbouring domains
(`adder_spaces.py`, `mul_spaces.py`, `div_spaces.py`, `sfu_spaces.py`,
`fma_dot_spaces.py`, `shift_simd_spaces.py`), the family and variant docs under
`knowledge/arch/approx/`, the notes of the three thinnest proposals (`liang2013`,
`venkatesan2011`, `kyaw2010`) and the approx gap files under
`knowledge/extract/gaps/`. Nothing in `chialu/spaces` changes here; every line
below is an instruction to the human who applies it.

All thirteen handles are already cited in `approx_spaces.py`, so no proposal
brings a new source. The question for each proposal is whether its mechanism is
represented as a family, as a choice value, or only as a sentence in a family doc.
`truncated_fixed_width` below is the `approx_mul_space` family (choices
`kept_guard_columns`, `correction`, `target`), which the bundle's existing-families
list omits although `approx_spaces.py` declares it; `mul_spaces.py` declares a family
of the same name with different choices, and the lines below target the approx one.

## absorbed

* `approximate_median_network (vasicek2015) -> approximate_logic_synthesis.method=cgp_evolution` — functional-level CGP over word-wide MIN/MAX nodes with heuristic seeding is that value (the variant doc narrates word-wide functional nodes and heuristic seeding, and `gaps/approximate_logic_synthesis.md` lists `candidate_representation=functional_level` and `population_seeding`); the 9- and 25-input median network is the benchmark circuit being approximated, and no registry domain holds comparator or sorting networks.
* `dynamic_voltage_accuracy_frequency_scaling (moons2017) -> approximate_mac_nn.precision_scaling=dvafs` — the variant doc narrates per-layer precision mapped onto subword lanes, frequency and supply; the subword reuse at constant word throughput is `mul.twin_precision_subword` / `shift.lane_width_gating.operation_packing`, and the voltage, frequency and power-domain coordination is a system-level control (the `couple_modes_to_dvfs` mutation of `accuracy_configurable`) rather than a datapath structure, so `throughput_policy` and `power_domain_partition` are not registered; `gaps/approximate_mac_nn.md` asks for a cross-unit DVAFS family, which is the human's alternative.
* `library_based_accelerator_dse (mrazek2019) -> error_analysis_quality.model=regression_selected` — the variant doc narrates autoAx's application-weighted library filtering and random-forest quality and cost models (mrazek2019 is cited there); the stochastic hill-climbing search with stagnation restarts and the final simulation/synthesis pass are an exploration procedure with no datapath structure, and the library side is the `export_pareto_library` mutation of `approximate_logic_synthesis`.
* `multi_level_approximate_accelerator_synthesis (zervakis2019) -> error_analysis_quality.model=regression_selected, composition_across_blocks=True` — the variant doc narrates the ANN trained on voltage-overscaled simulations that plays the regression model's role (zervakis2019 is cited there), and the `add_voltage_overscaling_axis` mutation is the VOS layer; the per-node Pareto binary search and the greedy voltage-island grouping are synthesis-flow and power-domain decisions; `gaps/approximate_logic_synthesis.md` (a DFG-level joint-selection `method` value) and `gaps/error_analysis_quality.md` (`model=ann`) already hold the finer-grained homes.

## new values

* `approximate_functional += choice operation: {divide, sqrt}` — under `method=dynamic_segment_exact_core`, AASR retains a 2k-bit window at the radicand's leading one (an even leading-one position is raised by one so the scale exponent is integral), an exact 2k-bit root core computes the reduced root, and the result shifts by (lA-2k+1)/2 with no auxiliary adder or subtractor (AASR-6 at 16 bits: ER 95.71%, NMED 5.33%, 76.5 against 222.4 um2 in ST 28 nm); `gaps/approximate_functional.md` lists the same axis as `operation_set` and `gaps/approximate_recurrence.md` as `operation: {divide, sqrt}` [jiang2019]
* `approximate_functional += choice exact_core: {restoring_array, lut, sequential}` — the reduced-width core is any exact combinational or sequential root or divider circuit; the alternative is the component slot `exact_core: div_space()` that `gaps/approximate_functional.md` requests, with `restoring_nonrestoring` and `digit_recurrence_sqrt_combined` as fillers [jiang2019]
* `approximate_functional.segment_or_lut_width: IntRange(3, 12) -> IntRange(3, 16)` — the pruned radicand width runs 2 to 32 bits in steps of 2, so k runs to 16 [jiang2019]
* `approximate_mac_nn += choice alphabet_size: {1, 2, 4, 8}` — the number of odd multiples the precomputer generates ({1}, {1,3}, {1,3,5,7}, all eight), which sets which weight quartets are representable, the size of the select/shift logic and the retraining constraint; the one-alphabet form removes the precomputer and the select logic (about 33% power and area at iso-speed against a conventional 12-bit neuron in 45 nm) [sarwar2018]
* `approximate_mac_nn += choice neurons_per_precomputer: IntRange(1, 4)` — Computation Sharing Multiplication shares one precomputer among up to four parallel neuron multipliers; 1 is a private precomputer [sarwar2018]
* `pp_perforation.cell += awtm_band_forced_block` — a b x b sub-product block whose low b/2 bits are exact, whose next b/2 bits are forced to one, and whose upper bits are exact with a carry predicted from the tallest partial-product column; it fills the AH x AL, AL x AH and AL x AL quadrants of a recursive 2b x 2b decomposition while AH x AH recurses exactly, and Wallace reduction sums the quadrants; it needs the `building_block_width` (extended to {2, 4, 8}) and `block_precision_map=mixed_precise_approximate` choices that `gaps/pp_perforation.md` lists for the recursively tiled multipliers [bhardwaj2014]
* `pp_perforation += choice carry_prediction: {two_or_more_threshold, simplified_or}` — the carry into the exact upper band is predicted by a two-or-more threshold over the tallest column or by a simplified OR; the other cells need a neutral value that the proposal does not name [bhardwaj2014]
* `pp_perforation += choice accuracy_modes: IntRange(1, 4)` — design-time modes 1 to 4 set how many AH x AH sub-multipliers of the recursion stay accurate while AHH x AHH always does (16 x 16 in Nangate 45 nm: area saving from 34.49% to 27.90% and mean error from 5.26% to 0.13% across the four modes); the bit-width-aware algorithm sizes the approximation region for the operand width [bhardwaj2014]
* `truncated_fixed_width += choice truncation_control: {design_time, runtime_column_gating}` — a runtime NT gates the partial products in the first kmax columns; the adder-side twin of this knob is `accuracy_configurable.reconfig_grain=truncation_width` (frustaci2019), and `gaps/truncated_fixed_width.md` asks for a dynamic-truncation family from the same paper [frustaci2020]
* `truncated_fixed_width += choice tree_mapping: {static, incremental_low_overhead}` — the reduction tree is built from the NT=kmax static tree by adding compressors for each higher precision, and the gated sum/carry bits are labelled, sorted and grouped by equal label so fewer compressors switch at low precision; evaluated on Wallace and Dadda 4:2 trees with a ripple-carry final adder, which are the slots `gaps/truncated_fixed_width.md` requests [frustaci2020]
* `truncated_fixed_width.correction += incremental_approximate_mean_offset` — AND-OR gates inject an NT-dependent mean-error offset in the partial-product generation stage, with constants for unsigned and signed forms (MED 11x lower than conventional dynamic truncation at NT=10, 16 x 16, iso-energy, 28 nm FDSOI) [frustaci2020]
* `pp_perforation += choice multiplicand_rounding_bit: IntRange(0, 14)` — PR|k,m rounds A at bit m before the retained modified-Booth partial products are generated, through Am* = Am xor a(m-1) and s*j = sj xor a(m-1), which costs one XOR-2 per encoder (the proposal's Int[0..n-1) at n = 16); `gaps/pp_perforation.md` lists the same axis as `partial_product_rounding_bit` and requires `partial_product_generation=mbe`; the family doc already narrates this line, and leon2018b is cited only under `error_analysis_quality`, so the handle is added to `pp_perforation.papers` [leon2018b]
* `pp_perforation += choice configuration_time: {design_time, runtime}` — DyPR gates operand bits with 2n multiplexers and a ROM indexed by the permitted MRED selects (k, m) (up to 47% energy saving at runtime against 69% at design time, n = 16, TSMC 65 nm); `gaps/accuracy_configurable.md` holds the same knob as `reconfig_grain=perforation_and_rounding` with `quality_selector=rom_indexed_mred`, so the human picks one home [leon2018b]
* `accuracy_configurable.reconfig_grain += signal_substitution_switch` — each substituted signal keeps its target logic and a mode-controlled switch selects target or substitute; substitutions are nested by quality constraint, so a tighter mode recovers a strict superset of a looser mode's errors; this differs from the gap file's `wire_by_switch` (tasoulas2020), which ties wires to constants [venkataramani2013]
* `accuracy_configurable += choice recovery: {none, selective_extra_cycle, universal_extra_cycle}` — TS/SS difference monitors (`error_detection=True`) accumulate, the clock extends by one cycle either only when a difference fires or in every accurate-mode cycle, the affected substitution circuits switch back to TS and downstream logic recomputes (two-cycle probability 0.002% to 0.24% across the benchmarks in IBM 45 nm); `power_gate_unused` covers the clock-extension power gating; `gaps/accuracy_configurable.md` asks instead for a generic quality-configurable synthesis family, which is the alternative because the family sits in `approx_adder_space` while SASIMI configures MAC, multiplier, SAD and ISCAS85 circuits [venkataramani2013]
* `dynamic_segment.unbiasing += poc_cascade_fill` — under `segment_select=static_msb_or_lsb`, the low product bits are not computed from partial products: cascading POC cells scan the low operand halves from MSB to LSB, the first position holding a 1 sets that and every lower product bit to 1, and preceding 00 positions produce 0, so the low half has no partial products and no carry path (12-bit ETM: 491 against 1028 um2 in 0.18 um, 50% to 96% less power); kyaw2010 is already in the family's papers, and because the fill replaces the LSB half rather than unbiasing a window, `truncated_fixed_width` with `truncation_location=operand_lsb` from its gap file is the alternative host [kyaw2010]
* `dynamic_segment += choice small_operand_fallback: Bool` — a control block and a multiplexer route operand pairs whose high halves are both zero to a conventional half-width multiplier, so small operands are exact [kyaw2010]

Three of these seven proposals were candidates for `new families`. `new values` is
preferred per the rules, and the deciding question is stated for each:
* `alphabet_set_multiplier` (sarwar2018): the precomputer/select/shift/add datapath is narrated by the `alphabet_set_shared` variant doc, but it lives as a label on a policy family, and `gaps/approximate_mac_nn.md` asks for a multiplier slot on `approximate_mac_nn`. A slot needs a fillable family, so the human who adds the slot registers `alphabet_set_multiplier` in `approx_mul_space` with the two choices above plus `final_addition: Bool` (the final addition disappears when one quartet covers the weight), and the two lines above become its choices. Evidence is one paper with no textbook or thesis block.
* `dynamic_column_truncation_multiplier` (frustaci2020): `gaps/truncated_fixed_width.md` proposes a dynamic-truncation family. The structure is column truncation plus correction plus a reduction tree, which is `truncated_fixed_width`, so the runtime knob and the tree mapping are choices on it, as `runtime_width_scaling` is on `dynamic_segment`. The handle sits in `dynamic_segment.papers` today, where the mechanism does not belong, and moves to `truncated_fixed_width.papers`.
* `quality_configurable_logic_synthesis` (venkataramani2013): `gaps/accuracy_configurable.md` proposes a generic quality-configurable family. The mode/detect/recover structure is `accuracy_configurable`'s, whose doc already covers GeAr's detect-and-recompute, and the family's adder-only placement is the gap file's known issue rather than this proposal's.

## new families

* none — no proposal describes a datapath structure absent from every family. The three closest candidates (the alphabet-set multiplier, the dynamic-truncation multiplier, the quality-configurable substitution circuit) are listed under `new values` with the deciding question, the adaptive square root is the existing `dynamic_segment_exact_core` method applied to a second operation, the AWTM block is a cell of the recursively tiled line that `gaps/pp_perforation.md` already sketches, and the two accelerator-level proposals are exploration flows.

## rejected

* `probabilistic_ripple_carry_adder (liang2013)` — the structure is `lower_part_approximate`'s exact-upper/approximate-lower partition (liang2013 is cited there); the lower cells are conventional full adders whose gates flip with a supply-dependent probability, which is a circuit/process technique, and the stochastic output has no frozen approximate algorithm model, while every approx family is `algorithm_level=True` and bit-exact against such a model; the `gate_error_rate: Real` axis also has no choice type in the space. If the human relaxes the contract, the representation is `lower_part_approximate.lower_cell += probabilistic_full_adder`.
* `truth_table_approximate_adder (venkatesan2011)` — MACACO's Adder1 to Adder4 are benchmark instances of the analysis method (`error_analysis_quality` cites venkatesan2011 and its variant docs narrate MACACO); the note gives only an erroneous-input count and a carry-interaction category with no cell structure or partition, and truth-table-altered cells are the `lower_part_approximate.lower_cell` zoo with the `cell_variant` sub-choice of `gaps/lower_part_approximate.md`.
