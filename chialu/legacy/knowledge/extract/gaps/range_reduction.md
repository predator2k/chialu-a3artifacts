# range_reduction: proposed changes to the space

* `method` values for function-specific identities and clipping (reciprocal/sqrt/2^x/log2 exact-preserving transforms, CORDIC prescaling identities, sigmoid endpoint clipping, logarithm exponent extraction) — several designs reduce by an algebraic identity rather than by any of the four listed methods [schulte_1994, walther_1971, delgado_frias_2000b, tang_1990, decaro_2009]
* `method` values for the exponential's relaxed quotient E of X/log 2 with bounded remainder (KCM implementation), the base-2 integer/fraction split, and the ln 2 quotient/remainder used by integer softmax — all are exp-specific reductions with a tolerated E error [pasca_2011, dedinechin_2010, hussain_2021, kim_2021]
* `method` values for split-constant subtraction with exact partial products and cancellation-triggered extra pieces, multiprecision subtraction of a long stored constant, and the 8087 DECOMPOSE plus fixed-step partial-REMAINDER instruction completed by a software loop [gal_1991, lynch_1995, palmer_1980]
* distinguish within `table_augmented` the reciprocal-table reduction z = y*r_i - 1, the high-radix digit-splitting table method, and the zero-cost uniform leading-bit interval split [dedinechin_2007, brisebarre_2005, pasca_2011]
* MRR choices `interval_form: {symmetrical, positive}`, `redundant_reduction: Bool`, `input_form: {fixed_point, floating_point}`, `first_reduction_architecture: {cellular_array, wallace_tree}`, `operand_recoding: {none, booth, modified_booth}`, and `multiplier_hardware_sharing: Bool` — modular reduction is parameterized by all of them [daumas_1995, muller_2016]
* Payne-Hanek choices `reduction_granularity: {full_period, half_period, quadrant, octant}`, `precision_extension: next_reciprocal_digit_on_demand`, `partial_product_scope: fractional_contributors_only`, and `reciprocal_generation: gosper_series` [payne_1983]
* high-radix table choices `argument_chunk_bits`, `remainder_components`, `regime_switching` thresholds, and precision parameter p, plus a slot for the Fast2sum expansion-compression stage [brisebarre_2005]
* choices `reduced_argument_representation: {one_word, double_word, triple_word}`, `reduction_steps: {one, two, repeated}`, and `constant_term_storage: {combined, separate_terms}` — the textbooks vary these independently of `method` [muller_2016, muller_2018]
* an output-compensation slot with `output_compensation_constraint: continuous_and_bijective`, `output_compensation_arity`, and `compensation_monotonicity` — correctly rounded library generation depends on inverting the compensation [lim_2021, lim_2021b]
* choice `path_structure: dual_close_far` for hardware reduction around x < 1/2 [detrey_2007, detrey_2007b]
* choices `table_rounding: truncate_down` for the reciprocal reduction table and `reconstruction_selector: quotient_or_exponent_bits` [ercegovac_2000, walther_1971]
