# hierarchical: proposed changes to the space

* choices `hierarchy_depth: Int` (2 in the reported experiments), `level_scheme: {uniform, powers_of_two_increasing, powers_of_two_decreasing, powers_of_two_increase_then_decrease}`, `hierarchy_selection: balanced_error_histogram_variance`, `inner_segmentation_policy: uniform`, and `outer_address_bits_selection: search_minimum_segment_count` — the family has no choices at all and these are the paper's design axes [lee_2009]
* out-of-family gaps in the bundle use the word hierarchical for other structures: a multiple-radix group-carry network for `carry_select.select_source`, hierarchical composition of prediction components in `segmented_carry_speculative`, and linear/hierarchical CPA composition [bedrij1962, ye2013, zimmermann1997#s04]
