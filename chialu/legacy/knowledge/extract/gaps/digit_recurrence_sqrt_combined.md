# digit_recurrence_sqrt_combined: proposed changes to the space

* per-operation `radix` (division 64 with square root 16 in one unit) — the two paths overlap different numbers of radix-4 iterations [bruguera_2020]
* `radix` values 512 and a 17-bit-digit (very high radix) value — speculation-based and prescaling-based combined units and the Cyrix multiplier-based recurrence use them [cortadella_1994, lang_1999, pasca_2011__s09]
* choice `stage_composition: {overlapped, cascaded}` with a component radix and iterations-per-cycle count — radix 16 and radix 64 are built from radix-4 or radix-8 sub-iterations in either arrangement [liu_2012, bruguera_2020, bruguera_2023]
* choice `microarchitecture_organization: {iterative, fully_pipelined}` — the pipelined unit replicates iteration logic per digit at about 2.5x area [bruguera_2023]
* choice `digit_iteration_sharing: {shared, separate}` and `first_iteration_handling` — registers can be shared while iteration datapaths stay separate, and the first iteration can be folded into initialization or prescaling [bruguera_2020]
* choice `comparison_lut_organization` (division base plus square-root offset table) — cuts selection storage by about 60 percent [bruguera_2023]
* choice `partial_root_3x_generation: {adder, on_the_fly}` — the 3x partial-root multiple can be produced by the converter [bruguera_2023]
* `digit_select` slot values beyond `qds_table`: parallel-comparator one-hot selection and rounding of a prescaled truncated residual [burgess_2007, lang_1999]
* choices for remainder-update speculation count, digit redundancy, partial-remainder encoding, and assimilated-MSB width — the VFP11 macrocell fixes all four [burgess_2007]
* slot or choice for the positive/negative on-the-fly root estimate representation [burgess_2007]
* choice `speculation_path: {narrow_selection_path, wide_residual_path}` — power differs by where speculation happens [liu_2012]
* choice `sqrt_recurrence_timing: {direct, retimed_postponed_square_term}` and `cycles_per_iteration` — square root can be retimed or split over two cycles to keep the division cycle time [cortadella_1994, lang_1999]
* choice `recurrence_prescaling: scale_residual_by_M` — prescaling keeps the scaled residual near 1 for rounding-based selection [lang_1999]
* choices `integer_divrem_support: Bool`, `recurrence_copies: {1,2,4,8}`, and early-termination conditions — integer divide/remainder can share the recurrence, and k copies retire rk bits per cycle [kim_2025]
* choice `internal_clock_ratio` or `double_pumped: Bool` — the recurrence can run twice per system clock [asprey_1993, hinton_2001]
* choice `shared_lookup_table: Bool` — a merged divide/square-root table addressed by three partial-remainder bits [gerwig_2004]
* a restoring/non-restoring recurrence-type choice covering the posit unit's one non-restoring iteration per cycle and the pencil-and-paper trial-digit methods [tiwari_2021, richards_1955__s11]
