# duplication: proposed changes to the space

* choice `decision_mechanism: {majority_vote, output_match}` and a `majority_voter` value for the `comparator` slot — triplicated schemes vote rather than compare two replicas, and `two_rail_tree` does not describe a voter [lala_2001#s07, von_neumann_1956#s01, townsend_2003]
* extend `replication` above 3 and add `standby_spares`, `decision_rule` (threshold vote such as 2-out-of-(n+3)), and `post_fault_mode: {remain_replicated, simplex, replace_with_spare}` — NMR and the STAR TARP array combine powered replicas with dormant spares and a replacement policy [lala_2001#s07, avizienis_1971, avizienis_gilley_1971]
* choice `replica_scope: {full_unit, selected_macros, function_only_heterogeneous, unit_set}` and a compared-state scope — DIVA duplicates function only, z196 duplicates exponent/control macros, G5 duplicates IU/FXU/FPU, and the 1946 report varies between register-plus-accumulator and accumulator-only comparison [austin_1999, carlough_2011, check_slegel_1999, burks1946]
* choices `communication_verification: in_order_reexecution`, `forward_progress_watchdog: Bool`, and `checker_datapath: heterogeneous_simple_algorithms` — the trailing-checker form adds mechanisms the lockstep form lacks [austin_1999]
* choice `recovery_policy: {stop_clock, correct_flush_restart, reset_restore_resume, checkpoint_freeze_ecc_repair, check_stop}` plus an array-specific clear/retry/disable policy — every commercial instance pairs the compare with a distinct recovery protocol [burks1946, austin_1999, check_slegel_1999, slegel_1999, schwarz_2002]
* `comparison_point` values for result receipt at single-copy cache/RU structures, DNN prediction versus likelihood-vector equality, and software checks before control-flow/memory instructions [check_slegel_1999, ozen_2025, sullivan_2018]
* choice `excluded_shared_arrays` — large arrays such as the branch target buffer are left outside the replicated unit and protected by parity [schwarz_2002]
* out-of-family gaps in the bundle target `carry_select.duplication` (time-multiplexed shared block, two-carry-chain/single-sum, shared A+B/AB functions, OR-derived second carry), `self_checking_datapath`, `multi_precision_simd_fma`, and `commercial_decimal_fpu` rather than this family [amelifard2005, asprey_1993, bedrij1962, tyagi1993, nicolaidis_2003, huang_2007, carlough_2011]
