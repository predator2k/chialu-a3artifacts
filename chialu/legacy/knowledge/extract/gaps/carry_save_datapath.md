# carry_save_datapath: proposed changes to the space

* `compressor` value `5_2` and a way to express mixed compressor use, per design or per recurrence stage — on-line units use [5:2] reduction, the packet-forwarding adder mixes 3:2 and 4:2, and the rotation-factor unit uses 4:2 for the sum of squares and 3:2 for the square root [ercegovac_2004#s01, nielsen_2000, ercegovac_lang_1988]
* choice `reduction_topology: {array, tree}` with a slot for the primitive counter/compressor cell and a `tree_construction: delay_aware_algorithmic` option — arrays trade speed for regularity, and delay-aware construction exploits the distinct full-adder input-to-output delays [zimmermann1997#s04, zimmermann1999, conway_nelson_2004]
* choices `carry_chunk_bits` (k) and `final_resolution: {zero_injection, pipelined_propagation}`, or a `partial_carry_save_radix` — partial carry-save in k-bit registered chunks is the FPGA form, and the exit is either latency or hardware [dedinechin_2008, muller_2018#s08]
* choice `partial_assimilation_width` — recurrences assimilate only the 3 to 6 leading bits digit selection needs, a 10-bit sign detector, or 8 bits per iteration [ercegovac_2004#s01, vazquez_2009b, davis_1969]
* choice `end_around_feedback: {direct, inverted}` — wrapping level carries back gives modulo 2^n - 1 or 2^n + 1 sums [zimmermann1999]
* choices `pipeline_operation_rate`, a limited-width vector-merging adder option and `carry_overflow_correction: modified_sign_full_adder` — full-adder-level pipelining, boundary merging and sign-position overflow correction are design decisions of chained carry-save DSP [noll_1991]
* choice `redundant_encoding: borrow_save` with `partial_compression_recoding: P_N_ppm_mmp` — borrow-save digits and partial recodings before leading-zero anticipation [nielsen_2000]
* choices for the number of serial CSAs and component-reducing termination of unused high/low positions [macsorley1961]
* `assimilator` slot admitting an unspecified generic carry-propagate adder and an in-memory (MAGIC NOR) assimilator [zimmermann1997#s03, salamat_2018]
* choices `negative_multiple_encoding: ones_complement_with_delayed_one`, `alignment_shift` (fixed 16-bit), `merged_secondary_subtract: Bool`, `redundant_rows: {2, 3}` with `feedback_form: {partial_sum, carry}`, and `multiplier_digit_recoding: signed_digit_radix_4` — instance-level options of the SRT remainder, the iterative accumulator, the AGU, the serial summator and the fused interpolator tree [edelman_1997, santoro1989, wijeratne_2007, dadda1965, pineiro_2005]
* slots in `rns_dsp_datapath`, `serial_serial_parallel`, `two_path.sig_adder` and `decimal_cordic_transcendental` that can be filled by `carry_save_datapath` [conway_nelson_2004, dadda1965, nielsen_2000, vazquez_2009b]
* choice `overflow_prediction: {none, toggle_detect_3bit_window}` — mantissa overflow is predicted from the three most significant bits of both carry-save vectors, without the addition, so the recovery shift happens inside the accumulation loop [vangal_2006]
