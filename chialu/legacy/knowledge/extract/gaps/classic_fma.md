# classic_fma: proposed changes to the space

* choice `case_organization: single_path` — the textbook algorithm superimposes product-anchored, addend-anchored, cancellation and subnormal cases on one path, which distinguishes it from multipath variants [muller_2018#s07]
* `negation_handling` values for two parallel adders with sign selection and for inversion followed by an increment shared with the rounding incrementer — the z990 uses two 116-bit adders computing A-B and B-A, and the 32 nm unit shares the negation and rounding increments [muller_2018#s08, schwarz_2005, kaul_2012]
* choice `addend_injection: free_7_3_counter_input` — the addend enters an unused input of the multiplier's compression structure [hokenek_1990]
* a `subnormal` slot with choices `internal_format_policy` (separate BFP/HFP representations) and `denormal_input_handling` (late Booth-term correction plus late aligner selection) — denormal operands change the aligner, exponent correction, normalization and exception paths [schwarz_2005, gerwig_2004]
* choice `unfinished_result_forwarding: stage6_unrounded_partially_normalized` — forwarding before rounding with later correction cuts dependent latency by one cycle [trong_2007]
* choice `increment_return_path: {addend_pipeline, multiplicand_pipeline}` — the rounding increment is performed through either pipeline according to the return path [montoye_1990]
* an initiation-interval choice — each POWER4 unit starts one FMA per cycle while the S/390 G5 multiply-then-add is nonpipelined [tendler_2002, schwarz_1999]
* choices `operation_set`, `integer_multiply_add_support`, `sign_variant_instructions` (fms/fnma) and `simd_width` — the FMAC hardware also executes integer XMA, sign variants and two-lane SIMD [sharangpani_2000, harrison_2000, maruyama_2010]
* choice `division_extension: newton_raphson_div_sqrt` and `newton_raphson.iter_mult` admitting `classic_fma` — tables, bypasses and control turn the FMA into the division/square-root primitive [liu_2012, cornea_1999]
* choice `posit_parameterization: nb, es` with posit extraction/packing — the posit generators make decode and repack part of the fused datapath [zhang_2019, tiwari_2021]
* choice `certainty_parallel_path: Bool` — exponent and certainty tracking run in parallel with the datapath [kaul_2012]
* `pipeline_depth` admitting half-cycle values — the POWER6 VFU reports a 6.5-cycle pipeline [eisen_2007]
* choice `multiply_array_passes: 1..2` — one pass per double-precision product in the PowerPC 604e and two through a half-size array in the PowerPC 603e, which is 19.5 mm2 against 11.7 mm2 of FPU [jessani_1998, jessani_1996]
* choice `addend_csa_stage_placement: {multiply_stage, add_stage}` — the 3:2 CSA group that injects the aligned addend sits in either stage [jessani_1998]
* choices `aligned_addend_partition_bits` and `alignment_constant_per_pass` — the dual-pass form splits the 161-bit aligned addend 26 low / 135 upper and offsets the shift count by 30 then 56, which turns the 161-bit adder into a 26-bit incrementer, an 81-bit CLA and a 54-bit incrementer [jessani_1998]
* choices `flush_subnormals` and `rounding_mode` on `classic_fma` — the CELL SPfpu flushes and truncates while the DPfpu computes denormal results in all four modes, the 603e separates a scientific from a real-time mode, and the support level costs 5 to 10 percent; `bf16_fma_datapath` already carries both choices [galal_2011, mueller_2005, oh_2006, jessani_1996]
* choices `denormal_operand_handling: {in_dataflow_correction, prenormalize_with_stall, trap_to_software}` and `prenormalization_scope: {special_cases_only, all_operands}` — Power3 prenormalizes the addend for the difficult cases, Power4 prenormalizes all operands at 3 cycles per extra denormal operand, and the latest zSeries traps on the disjoint case [schwarz_2003]
* choice `overflow_on_max_exponent: saturate` beside the rounding-mode set — the CELL units treat the maximum exponent as a binade of normal numbers and saturate on overflow, with an FPSCR flag marking any deviating result [mueller_2005, oh_2006]
* an `exp` slot for `exponent_path` — the exponent path computes the shift amounts, the overflow and underflow conditions and the result exponent, it carries POWER7's two most critical paths, and the CELL exponent rounder alone gained 15 FO4 from optimization [boersma_2011, oh_2006]
* choice `internal_exponent_rebiasing: target_emin_biased_twos_complement` — rebiasing the 13-bit intermediate exponent with the target precision's eminT makes underflow the sign bit and removes the 13-bit comparators [boersma_2011]
* choices `forwarded_result_format: {rounded_internal_representation, unrounded_with_multiplier_fixup}` and `rounding_target_precision: instruction_selected_sp_or_dp` — POWER7 forwards a fully rounded result at 5.5 cycles where POWER6 forwarded an unrounded one, and every scalar operation rounds to SP or DP on 64-bit operands [boersma_2011]
* choice `pipeline_style: {automatic_retiming, guided_retiming, replicated_half_pumped}` with `pipeline_depth: 3..16` — the generator retimes automatically, retimes with one stage pinned after the Booth select signals, or replicates the multiplier for a multicycle path [galal_2013]
* choice `clock_gating: per_stage_enable_plus_block_gating_by_instruction` — stages are clocked only for valid instructions and blocks are gated by opcode and by operand values, which disables the multiplier on adds and the incrementer and compound adder on the addend/product alignment [galal_2013, oh_2006, mueller_2005]
* choice `fma_operand_order: {a_times_b_plus_c, a_times_c_plus_b}` — the operand order is what removes the multiplexer from the multiplier inputs [mueller_2005]
* choice `subsume_integer_mac: bool` — 16x16b signed and unsigned integer multiply-adds reuse the single-precision multiplier after an extra formatter stage, at one more cycle of latency [mueller_2005]
* choice `datapath_folding_rows: 2..3` — folding the wide intermediate fraction to fit a narrow floorplan reshapes the adder's carry-lookahead balancing, the LZA edge vector and the normalization shifter's stages [mueller_2005]
* a rounding-contract choice on the FMA families — a MAF is called IEEE compatible when it delivers the result of a sequential FMPY and FADD, which one rounding of the full-precision product and sum does not [quach_1991]
