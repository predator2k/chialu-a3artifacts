# srt_high_radix: proposed changes to the space

* extend `radix` with 64, 128, 256, and 512 — cascaded, overlapped, and speculative designs reach these effective radices [atkins_1968, bruguera_2020, cortadella_1994, kornerup_2005, oberman_1997]
* choice `quotient_digit_set` or `maximum_digit_a` with `beta/2 <= a <= beta-1` — the explicit set, not only its redundancy class, fixes the divisor multiples, the selection intervals, and the valid table parameters [robertson_1958, taylor_1985, edelman_1997, pratt_1995, rowen_1988, clarke_1996, kornerup_2005, burgess_1995]
* choice `residual_form: {irredundant, carry_save, borrow_save_signed_digit}` and `carry_storage_granularity` (one carry bit per four sum bits) — the residual representation is part of the selection path and of register cost [coe_1995, pratt_1995, taylor_1985, burgess_1995, gerwig_2004, chen2018]
* choices `base_stage_radix: {2, 4}`, `stage_composition: {cascaded, overlapped_quotient_selection, overlapped_remainder_computation, range_reduction}`, `overlap_organization: {cascaded, pairwise, partial, maximal}`, and `quotient_digit_decomposition: qj = k qH + qL` — `overlapped_stages` alone does not say what is overlapped or how the radix-16 digit splits [atkins_1968, taylor_1985, harris_1997, oberman_1997, lang_1999, lang_2007, lang_2007b, bruguera_2020]
* `digit_select` values beyond `qds_table`: `sign_and_threshold_comparisons`, `prescaled_direct_integer_part`, `table_speculation`, and `arithmetic_approximation`, plus `generated_digit_subset: Bool` for speculative omission of quotient values [robertson_1958, chen2018, cortadella_1994]
* choices for table truncation: divisor bits `b`/`u`, residual integer/fraction digits `t`/`f`, asymmetric sum/carry precision `p`/`n`, `qds_input_architecture: {redundant_inputs, assimilated_remainder}`, and index quantization — these fix table size, reachability, and validity [burgess_1995, kornerup_2005, edelman_1997, oberman_1998]
* choice `prescaling: Bool` with `first_digit_placement: pre_processing_parallel` — prescaling is a mutation but no choice records it or where the first digit is formed [muller_2018, bruguera_2020, chen2018, lang_1999]
* choice `quotient_accumulation: {separate_positive_negative, on_the_fly}` — Pentium and the verified radix-4 core keep R/L registers and subtract at the end [pratt_1995, clarke_1996, oberman_1997]
* choices `circuit_style: {static_cmos, skew_tolerant_dual_rail_domino}` and `internal_clock_ratio: 2x` — circuit style gives 1.5x to 1.7x and a doubled recurrence clock gives four bits per system cycle from radix-4 hardware [harris_1997, asprey_1993]
* choice `table_encoding: {gray, folded, line, choose_highest}` — encoding and folding move table delay and area by tens of percent [oberman_1998]
