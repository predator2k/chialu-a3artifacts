# piecewise_poly: proposed changes to the space

* choices segment_pairing (adjacent_pairs), continuity_orders, adjacent_segment_constraints (shared_constant_and_linear), and coefficient_quantization_solver (mixed_integer_programming) — sharing constrained coefficients between adjacent segments removes one stored coefficient per pair and cuts the ROM by 30 to 50%. [decaro_2009, strollo_2011]
* slots for an explicit squarer and for the fused fixed-width carry-save evaluator, with choices squarer_output {exact, rounded, truncated} and partial_product_matrix {full, truncated} — quadratic evaluators are built around these components and their truncation is part of the error budget. [decaro_2009, decaro_2017]
* basis value shifted_monomial n_j + m_j(x - a_j) + p_j(x - a_j)^2 and a local_coordinate_coefficient_transform choice — evaluators work in the segment-local coordinate and transform coefficients to reduce dynamic range. [decaro_2017, lee_2009]
* choice constraint_formulation (integer_linear_programming) with coefficient_priority {linear_then_quadratic, quadratic_then_linear} — the ILP combines every error source and the two-pass order fixes which coefficient is minimized first. [decaro_2017]
* coefficient_optimization values least_squares (with power-of-two C search), exhaustive_ulp_adjustment / exhaustive_coefficient_adjustment with one-bit width trial, three-pass coefficient_rounding_compensation, constrained_fpminimax_plus_neighbor_width_search, and rounding_interval_linear_programming — each is the central coefficient-selection mechanism of a cited design and none is in the enumeration. [delgado_frias_2000b, zhang_1996, schulte_1994, walters2005, pineiro_2005, pasca_2011__s08, lim_2021, lim_2021b]
* segments domain extended to 2, 7, 19, 28, 80, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, and 8192, and freed from the multiple-of-four step — reported designs use all of these counts. [zhang_1996, delgado_frias_2000b, lee_2009, pineiro_2005, schulte_1994, walters2005, strollo_2011, pasca_2011__s08]
* degree domain extended beyond 4 — CRLIBM's logarithm uses degree 12 over 8 subintervals and correctly rounded software pieces use degrees 3 through 9. [muller_2016__s13, lim_2021, lim_2021b]
* choices term_realization {simple_rom, power_and_multiply}, powering_unit {table_based, ad_hoc_partial_product}, symmetry_exploitation, and per_term_precision_tuning — the developed-form evaluator mixes ROM terms with power-and-multiply terms, folds segments around their midpoint, and tunes precision per term. [detrey_2005]
* choice term_reduction {separate_multioperand_adder, merged_carry_save_tree} — partial products and coefficients can share one carry-save tree with a single final adder. [schulte_1994]
* coefficient-table slot through which compressed_lut fills T0 storage (zero_order_coefficient_compression) — T0 is about half the table and can be replaced by representative-plus-difference tables. [hsiao_2014]
* range_reducer value none — compound functions are evaluated directly over a supplied interval without reduction. [lee_2009]
* choices segmentation_selection (trial_and_error) and subdomain_addressing (common_prefix_following_bits) with counterexample-guided constraint sampling — software piecewise generators pick pieces by trial and index sub-domains by bits after the common prefix. [lim_2021, lim_2021b]
* coeff_encoding value for coefficients stored as sums of two double-precision numbers — CRLIBM's first two logarithm coefficients are double-double. [muller_2016__s13]
* choice output_rounding_bias (function_specific_exhaustive) — a bias selected by exhaustive simulation is injected before truncation. [pineiro_2005]
* choices input_notation {sign_magnitude, twos_complement} and out_of_range_policy (force_to_zero_or_one) — the two-segment sigmoid implementation depends on the input notation and clamps outside its range. [zhang_1996]
* choice arithmetic_precision_configuration {full_fp32, reduced_A_mantissa, reduced_A_mantissa_and_BFP24_C} — reduced multiplier/adder widths trade ulps for resources in the tensor-block sigmoid. [langhammer_2021]
* choice coefficient_source (hardware_provided) for a chained multiply-add instruction — Sparc64 VIIIfx supplies each coefficient from hardware to a software-visible piecewise recurrence. [maruyama_2010]
* choice evaluation_seriality (pipelined_bit_serial) — the neural-network generators evaluate bit-serially at 14 to 28 machine cycles per operation. [delgado_frias_2000b]
* rounding_contract value for a total absolute error below 1 ulp with separately budgeted terms — several designs specify a maximum absolute error rather than faithful or exact rounding. [hsiao_2014, walters2005]
