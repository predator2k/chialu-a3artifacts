## summary
The document proposes a modified Svoboda division algorithm for minimally redundant signed-digit arithmetic, with divisor prescaling followed by deterministic quotient-digit generation from one partial-remainder digit and its transfer digit (pp.887-889). A radix-16 working example demonstrates the algorithm (p.889).

## families
### svoboda_tung  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Step 1 multiplies the normalized divisor and dividend by constant factors until the divisor has the form \(1+e\), with \(e\) bounded so an erroneous trial digit can be compensated in one step. Step 2 uses the leading partial-remainder digit and its carry/borrow to generate each quotient digit without comparing divisor multiples against the partial remainder (pp.887-889).

errors_and_checks: The relation \(x^0=q(1+e)+d=qy+d\) verifies the division. The derived bounds on \(e\) ensure that a mistaken prospective quotient digit can be compensated in the immediately following step. No hardware fault-detection coverage or numerical error rate is reported.   # pp.887-888

### generalized_signed_digit  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Each radix-\(r\) digit represents a signed integer from a redundant digit set. Addition/subtraction forms an interim digit and a neighboring transfer digit before combining them, so carry propagation does not extend across the operand and operation time is independent of operand length (p.887).
