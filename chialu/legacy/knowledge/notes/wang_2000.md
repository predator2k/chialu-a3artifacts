## summary
The paper proposes two 32-bit priority encoders that use a three-level look-ahead structure implemented in NP Domino CMOS logic. The low-power design replaces parallel-connected dynamic gates on less critical paths with series-connected gates and adds a circuit that resolves the resulting race condition. The reported comparison shows simultaneous speed, layout-area, and power improvements over a conventional simple-look-ahead encoder.

## families
### priority_encoder  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The encoder updates one-hot priority tokens through three look-ahead levels rather than through a fully sequential kill chain. Four outputs share a first-level look-ahead signal, the second level connects two 4-bit cells, and the third level connects two 8-bit macro cells. Newhs32 uses parallel-connected NP Domino dynamic gates. Newhslp32 retains the look-ahead hierarchy but uses series-connected dynamic gates on less critical paths to reduce switching activity; added pMOS devices correct outputs after the later look-ahead signal arrives. # pp.1511-1513

## space_gaps
* `priority_encoder.output_form` needs a `one_hot_only` value because the paper generates one active output without a documented binary-encoding stage. # p.1511
* `priority_encoder` lacks a choice for static/dynamic logic style, including NP Domino CMOS. # pp.1511-1513
* `priority_encoder` lacks choices for parallel-connected versus series-connected dynamic evaluation networks and for race-correction circuitry. # p.1512
* A single `lookahead_group` value does not fully express the paper’s hierarchy of 4-bit cells, 8-bit macros, and three look-ahead levels. # pp.1511-1512
