## summary
The paper proposes non-iterative and iterative Mitchell logarithmic multipliers whose mantissa additions use LOA/MAA3/SOA inexact adders and truncated binary-logarithm converters. Selected 16-bit designs reduce NMED by up to 18% and PDP by up to 37% against logarithmic multipliers built from exact units. Approximate Booth multipliers provide smaller errors, while non-iterative ALMs target lower-power applications that tolerate larger errors.

## families
### logarithmic  (role: extends)
mechanism: ALM-LOA/ALM-MAA3/ALM-SOA retain Mitchell leading-one detection, binary-to-logarithm conversion, logarithmic-domain mantissa addition and logarithm-to-binary conversion. ALM-LOA replaces the lower mantissa-adder bits with OR gates. ALM-MAA3 copies one operand into the lower sum bits. ALM-SOA sets the lower sum bits to one to offset Mitchell’s negative error and truncates the corresponding binary-to-logarithm bits. # p.2-4
choices:
  base: mitchell   # p.2
  correction: none   # p.3-4
  mantissa_adder: truncated (ALM-LOA/ALM-MAA3); set_one_soa (ALM-SOA)   # p.3-4
new_choices:
  binary_log_converter_precision: accurate | truncated_by_M_bits — ALM-SOA truncates the converter by the same width used by SOA; LOA/MAA3 retain accurate conversion   # p.3-4
slots:
  log_adder: lower_part_approximate [lower_cell=or_gate | approx_mirror_ama | set_one_soa] [outside slot domain]   # p.3-4

### logarithmic  (role: extends)
mechanism: IALM computes Mitchell’s omitted correction product with a second logarithmic multiplication and adds the correction to the original product. IALM-S uses SOA/TBLC for Adder1 and Adder2 and an exact Adder3. IALM-SL and IALM-SM replace Adder3 with LOA and MAA3 respectively. An estimator records Adder1’s carry and selects either x1/x2 or their one’s complements for the correction. # p.3-5

### lower_part_approximate  (role: extends)
mechanism: LOA computes the lower m sum bits with OR gates and supplies a[m−1]b[m−1] to the exact upper adder. MAA3 copies b[m−1:0] into the lower sum and supplies a[m−1] as carry. SOA sets all lower sum bits to one and supplies a[m−1]b[m−1], which compensates the negative error of Mitchell multiplication. # p.3-4
choices:
  lower_cell: or_gate (LOA); approx_mirror_ama (MAA3); set_one_soa [outside domain] (SOA)   # p.3-4
  carry_to_upper: msb_and (LOA/SOA); a[m−1] [outside domain] (MAA3)   # p.3-4
new_choices:
  none
slots:
  upper_adder: none
parameters: n-bit adder with m inexact lower bits and an (n−m)-bit exact upper adder   # p.3-4
results:
| metric | value | unit | technology / device | baseline | condition | page |
| SOA ER | 1−(1/2)^m | probability | UNKNOWN / 2018 | exact lower addition | m inexact bits | p.4 |
| SOA MED | 2^m/3−1/(3×2^m) | error distance | UNKNOWN / 2018 | exact lower addition | m inexact bits | p.4 |
| SOA WCE | 2^(m−1) | error distance | UNKNOWN / 2018 | exact lower addition | m inexact bits | p.4 |
errors_and_checks: LOA has ER=1−(3/4)^m, MED=3×2^(m−4)−1/8 and WCE=2^(m−1). MAA3 has ER=1−(1/2)^m, MED=2^(m−2) and WCE=2^(m−1). # p.3
conditions: Accurate LOD/BLC units are retained with LOA/MAA3. TBLC is permitted with SOA because the corresponding sum bits are forced to one. # p.3-4
evidence: §III.A and Figs. 3-5, p.3-4

## space_gaps
* The logarithmic family’s `log_adder` slot excludes `lower_part_approximate`, although LOA/MAA3 fill that role in the implemented ALMs. # p.3-5
* The `lower_cell` domain lacks `set_one_soa`, whose lower outputs are fixed to one. # p.4
* The `mantissa_adder` domain collapses LOA and MAA3 into `truncated`, although the paper reports distinct sum/carry rules and error behavior. # p.3-4

## open_questions
* The paper calls the upper portions and IALM-S Adder3 “exact” without identifying their carry-propagate microarchitecture. # p.3-5
