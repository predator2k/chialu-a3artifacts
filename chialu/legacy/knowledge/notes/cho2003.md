## summary
The paper proposes a W-bit-input/W-bit-output fixed-width two's-complement squarer that derives compensation from modified Booth encoder outputs and partitions discarded partial products by error significance. The proposed error is close to rounding and below truncation, while fixed-width implementation reduces area and power relative to the full-width squarer. # pp.213, 216-218

## families
### squarer  (role: extends)

The fixed-width implementation retains the W-bit quantized result from the ideal (2W-1)-bit square and omits hardware for the W-1 least-significant product bits. # pp.213-214

### truncated_fixed_width  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The partial-product array is divided into retained MP and discarded LP regions, and LP is divided into major/minor groups by contribution to quantization error. The bias adds the exact carry from LP_major, an approximate carry derived from the expected LP_minor contribution, and one for rounding. Booth encoder zero/nonzero signals generate the approximate carry without multiplier coefficients. ACGP I derives Boolean functions from carry thresholds; ACGP II replaces large Karnaugh maps with staged full-adder grouping. # pp.214-217

## space_gaps
* The `truncated_fixed_width` family lacks a choice for compensation derived from Booth encoder outputs rather than multiplier coefficients. # pp.213, 215
* The `truncated_fixed_width` family lacks a choice for major/minor partitioning of discarded partial products. # pp.213-215
* The `truncated_fixed_width` family lacks a choice for ACGP I versus staged-full-adder ACGP II approximate-carry generation. # pp.216-217
* The `squarer` family lacks an output-width policy for fixed-width squaring. # p.213
