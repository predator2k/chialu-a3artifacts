## summary
The paper reports the physical/process, cache, power-management, clocking, and execution characteristics of AMD's Zen CCX. The integer unit executes four ALU and two AGU operations, while the 128b FPU executes two MUL and two ADD operations, but the paper does not disclose their arithmetic microarchitectures (p.52).
