## summary
The document presents a 3-cycle variable-precision FMA that executes one 24b-mantissa operation, two 12b-mantissa operations, or four 6b-mantissa operations with single-cycle throughput. Certainty tracking bounds result accuracy and triggers recomputation at higher precision when needed. The fabricated 32nm CMOS unit reaches 52GFLOPS/W for single precision and 162GFLOPS/W for 4-way 6b operation at 1.05V.

## families
### multi_precision_simd_fma  (role: proposes)
mechanism: One mantissa datapath is partitioned at runtime into one 24b, two 12b, or four 6b lanes. The partial-product tree disables off-diagonal products, the sparse-tree adder resets boundary carries, and the alignment/normalization shifters and LZA operate as independent lane segments. A variable-precision incrementer accepts four independent increment signals. A parallel certainty path propagates a 5b accuracy field and requests recomputation in 12b or 24b mode when a lower-precision result is uncertain.

### classic_fma  (role: extends)
mechanism: Addend alignment runs in parallel with a radix-4 Booth multiplier during cycle one. A 3:2 compressor, 48b sparse-tree adder, and 24b incrementer combine the product and addend during cycle two while an LZA estimates normalization. Cycle three normalizes to 24b and performs IEEE rounding. Mantissa negation uses inversion followed by an increment shared with the rounding incrementer.

### booth_recoded_parallel  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: A 24b×24b unsigned radix-4 Booth-encoded multiplier operates beside the addend alignment shifter. One unified partial-product reduction tree supports all precision modes by resetting off-diagonal partial products for independent 12b and 6b products. A product recentering certainty term, ΔA×ΔB = 2Ep-Ua-Ub, is injected into the appropriate compressor-tree column.
