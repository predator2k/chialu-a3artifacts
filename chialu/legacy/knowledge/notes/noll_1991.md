## summary
The paper extends carry-save arithmetic from multiplier arrays to pipelined inner products, recursive DSP, saturation, division, modulo multiplication, and CORDIC. Carry propagation is postponed by representing each intermediate result as sum/carry vectors and applying a vector-merging adder only where conventional representation is required. Limited-MSD estimates permit wordlength-independent saturation and decision-directed iterations, with explicit correction for uncertain decisions. (pp.121-138)

## families
### carry_save_datapath  (role: extends)
mechanism: Each intermediate value is represented by sum and carry vectors, with carries stored and passed diagonally between adder rows. One-dimensional full-adder-level pipelining replaces carry propagation inside cascaded operations. A vector-merging adder performs the postponed carry propagation at the end or at boundaries where operating twice on sum/carry vectors would cost more. (pp.122-125)

### carry_save_array  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Inner-product implementations reorder partial products into bit planes or modified bit planes and pipeline the full-adder array with sum/carry outputs retained between stages. Distributed half-period latches permit two full additions per period. Fixed coefficients use hardwired shifts and canonical-signed-digit recoding. (pp.128-133)

### saturating_clamp  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Saturation is detected from a limited number of the most significant sum/carry digits without resolving the complete carry chain. For two inspected digits, Boolean tests force positive or negative sum/carry saturation patterns. A limited vector-merging adder generalizes the estimate to more inspected digits. (pp.127-128)
choices:
  detect: top_bits_or_reduce   # p.127
  clamp: bitwise_force   # p.127
new_choices:
  inspected_msd_digits: 2 — number of leading sum/carry digits used for the illustrated saturation detector   # pp.127-128
slots:
  none
parameters: p = 2 illustrated; p = m gives exact detection; saturation interval ±0.5   # pp.127-128
results: none reported
errors_and_checks: With p = 2, saturation is uncertain over the printed positive/negative intervals. The uncertainty disappears only when all m digits participate in carry propagation. (pp.127-128)
conditions: Approximate in-loop saturation is suitable when limited-MSD detection suffices. An exact saturation after the out-of-loop vector-merging adder can remove the uncertainty. (p.128)
evidence: §3.3; Fig. 7; Eqs. (9)-(11), pp.127-128

### srt_radix2  (role: extends)
mechanism: A modified radix-2 nonrestoring divider retains the partial remainder in carry-save form and estimates its sign from three most significant digits. The uncertain estimate selects quotient digit zero, allowing later iterations to counterbalance the remainder. Positive/negative quotient digits are accumulated separately and merged after the iterations. (pp.135-137)

evidence: §4.3.1; Figs. 18-19; Eqs. (14)-(21), pp.135-137

### redundant_cordic  (role: extends)
mechanism: Coordinates and remaining angle use carry-save representation, while limited-MSD inspection estimates the angle sign. Uncertain rotations cannot use a zero digit because that would change the fixed scale factor. Replicated angle-sequence elements allow an incorrect rotation to be counterbalanced in subsequent stages. (p.138)

## new_families
### carry_save_modulo_multiplier  (domain: redundant: signed-digit arithmetic, closest: carry_save_datapath, why_not: the vocabulary lacks an iterative modular multiplier whose quotient multiple is selected from a limited carry-save estimate)
mechanism: Iterative partial-product modulo additions retain the partial remainder in carry-save form. Four or five leading digits estimate the remainder and select the modulus multiple, reducing the per-iteration carry chain from the full wordlength to the inspected prefix. Final carry propagations occur after the last iteration and can overlap the next operation. (p.137)

## space_gaps
* carry_save_datapath lacks choices for pipeline operation rate, limited-width vector-merging adders, and carry-overflow correction. (pp.123-127)
* saturating_clamp lacks a limited-MSD/vector-merging detector value and an explicit uncertainty contract. (pp.127-128)
* srt_radix2 permits only qds_table in digit_select, while this design uses a short ripple adder and comparison against -0.25. (pp.136-137)
