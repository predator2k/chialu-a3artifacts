## summary
The paper proposes HFP8, which uses 1-4-3 FP8 with exponent bias 4 for forward propagation and 1-5-2 FP8 with loss scaling for backward propagation. The paper also proposes residual-feedback FP8 weight updates and quantization after maximum subtraction for an 8-bit SoftMax path. Experiments report near-FP32 accuracy across image/language/speech/object-detection workloads and estimated end-to-end training-time improvements.

## families
### fp8_training_datapath  (role: extends)
mechanism: Weights and activations use 1-4-3 FP8 with exponent bias 4 during forward propagation. Errors and gradients use 1-5-2 FP8 during backpropagation because gradients require more dynamic range. Auto-adjusted loss scaling makes activation gradients representable. The dual-format FPU supports both formats with an estimated area increase relative to a 1-5-2-only FPU.

### softmax_layernorm  (role: extends)
mechanism: The last FC output is kept at 1-6-9 precision until SoftMax subtracts xmax. Quantization to 1-4-3 FP8 then occurs after x-xmax, which moves the largest SoftMax inputs near zero where the floating-point representation has greater fidelity. The paper states that this ordering permits SoftMax computation in 8 bits.
choices:
  max_subtraction: true   # p.6
new_choices:
  quantization_point: after_max_subtraction — selects whether conversion to FP8 precedes or follows x-xmax   # p.6

## new_families
### round_off_residual_weight_update  (domain: dot, closest: fp8_training_datapath, why_not: the mechanism is an optimizer/update accumulator with residual feedback and distributed partitioning rather than an FP8 product-accumulation datapath)
mechanism: Each learner updates one Nth of the weights after reduce-scatter. Each FP8 weight update subtracts the gradient and the preceding round-off residual, quantizes the new weight to FP8, computes the resulting quantization residual, and stores that residual locally in FP16. All-gather distributes FP8 weights rather than FP16 gradients, which reduces communication and local memory traffic.
choices: weight_format: {fp8}; residual_format: {fp16}; residual_feedback: Bool; update_partition: {full_model, one_over_n}; all_gather_payload: {gradients, weights}
