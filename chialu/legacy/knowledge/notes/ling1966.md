## summary
The paper proposes a binary parallel adder that propagates a complementing signal rather than a carry signal. The adder forms preliminary sum bits and complement/inhibit controls simultaneously from adjacent operand bits, but the paper reports no measured delay, area, or power.

## families
### ling_prefix  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The method decomposes the sum as S = Tn + Pn, where Tn contains left-shifted anbn terms and Pn contains an ⊕ bn terms. All 1's of Pn are inserted into Tn. Ln identifies extraction positions; Ln and Tn then activate either an inhibit circuit or a complementing circuit. The propagated complementing pulse inverts the preliminary bits that require carry effects, while diodes prevent backward transmission. The circuits for Sk, Ck, and Ik depend on bits k and k-1 and are formed simultaneously from the operands.

## open_questions

* The paper does not state whether the illustrated complementing line is buffered or segmented.
* The 16-bit example demonstrates operation but does not establish a fixed supported operand width.
