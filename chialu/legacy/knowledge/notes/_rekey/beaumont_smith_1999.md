## summary
The paper implements a 3-cycle IEEE-754 fp64 adder that merges rounding with significand addition through a flagged prefix adder. The paper extends the design with 2-cycle accumulation by feeding back an unnormalised, correctly rounded result and its normalisation distance. The paper also proposes a 2-cycle near/far-path adder with a significand-MSB prediction scheme that removes one speculative adder.

## families
### single_path  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Stage 1 performs exponent subtraction/significand swap/unpacking/alignment, stage 2 performs significand addition and merged rounding, and stage 3 performs post-normalisation. A flagged prefix adder produces the ordinary/plus-1/plus-2 significand alternatives within the addition stage. An LZA predicts the true-subtraction normalisation distance concurrently with addition, and a shared correction multiplexer handles a one-bit LZA error or a true-addition overflow. (p.2–4)

### compound_flagged_prefix  (role: extends)
mechanism: A row of half adders converts kill-generate strings into propagate strings before a modified parallel-prefix carry tree. The prefix tree generates flag bits that identify result bits invertible for incrementing a two's-complement result. Flagged inversion cells select the ordinary/plus-1/plus-2 result; specialised logic replaces the two least-significant flagged inversion cells to support plus 2. (p.3)

### two_path  (role: proposes)
mechanism: A far path handles all true additions and true subtractions with exponent difference at least 2. A near path handles true subtraction with exponent difference 0 and selected difference-1 cases that can require large left normalisation. Exponent LSBs trigger speculation, while significand MSBs determine which operand is shifted right by one bit. A late multiplexer selects the near/far result after pipeline stage 2. (p.5–6)

## new_families
### unnormalised_feedback_fp_accumulator  (domain: dot, closest: streaming_accurate_accumulator, why_not: the feedback carries an unnormalised IEEE result plus an LZA distance rather than a fixed-point window or compensated sum)
mechanism: The second pipeline stage feeds back a correctly rounded but possibly unnormalised significand together with its exponent/sign/normalisation distance. The next accumulation overlaps normalisation of the feedback operand with alignment against the new addend. Bypass multiplexers select whether the feedback significand bypasses normalisation, bypasses alignment after a partial left shift, or is normalised while the addend is aligned. (p.4–5)

## space_gaps
* `compound_flagged_prefix.outputs` lacks a value for the paper's ordinary/plus-1/plus-2 and complemented result set. (p.3)
* `single_path` lacks a choice that records rounding merged into the significand prefix tree. (p.2–3)
* `two_path` lacks a choice for asymmetric exponent-difference-1 dispatch using exponent LSBs and significand MSBs. (p.5–6)
