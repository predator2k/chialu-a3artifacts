## summary
The document proposes PR|k,m, which combines partial-product perforation with multiplicand rounding in a signed modified-Booth multiplier. (pp.42-43) DyPR exposes k and m at runtime and selects configurations through a ROM containing predetermined MRED values. (p.44) The evaluated PR configurations form the reported energy-MRED Pareto frontier against the examined approximate multipliers. (pp.47-48)

## families
### pp_perforation  (role: extends)
mechanism: PR|k,m omits the k least significant modified-Booth partial products and discards the m-1 least significant bits of A. The retained part Am is incremented by am-1, while Am* = Am XOR am-1 and s*j = sj XOR am-1 generate the rounded signed partial products with one extra XOR-2 gate in the encoder. An accurate Wallace tree accumulates the retained products, correction term, and sign factors. (pp.42-43)

### accuracy_configurable  (role: extends)
mechanism: DyPR drives each operand bit through a multiplexer so runtime k/m controls can replace omitted bits with zero. A ROM maps each k/m configuration to its predetermined MRED, which permits selection from an application-supplied error bound. The multiplexers can be implemented as AND-2 gates. (p.44)
