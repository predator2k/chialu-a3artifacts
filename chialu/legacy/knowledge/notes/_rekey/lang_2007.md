## summary
The document extends an earlier radix-10 digit-recurrence divider into one unit that performs radix-10 or radix-16 division. The combined recurrence shares quotient selection, a radix-2 two's-complement MS-slice, carry-save adders, conversion, and rounding across the two modes (pp.967-970). Synthesis reports nearly unchanged cycle time and about 20% less area than separate radix-10 and radix-16 units (p.970).

## families
### decimal_digit_recurrence  (role: extends)
mechanism: The radix-10 recurrence decomposes each quotient digit as qj = 5qHj + qLj, where qHj belongs to {-1,0,1} and qLj belongs to {-2,-1,0,1,2}. The most-significant recurrence slice and quotient selection operate in radix-2 two's complement, while the remaining recurrence uses BCD digit vectors. Precomputed 5dBCD/2dBCD multiples, dual-radix CSAs, signed-digit conversion, and rounding complete the decimal path (pp.967-969).

### srt_high_radix  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The radix-16 recurrence decomposes each quotient digit as qj = 4qHj + qLj, with both qHj and qLj selected from {-2,-1,0,1,2}. The selection function is decomposed into two radix-4 selections. Selection constants are preloaded and compared against truncated residual/divisor estimates, while qL is computed speculatively for each possible qH value and selected by a 5:1 mux (pp.967-969).

### dual_radix_digit_recurrence  (domain: decimal, closest: decimal_digit_recurrence, why_not: decimal_digit_recurrence does not express a runtime-selectable radix-10/radix-16 recurrence with shared hardware)
