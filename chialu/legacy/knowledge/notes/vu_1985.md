## summary
The document proposes fractional and quotient-remainder representations that replace addition modulo a large arbitrary M with binary addition and small lookup tables. The fractional representation supports exact sign detection, while the quotient-remainder representation converts residues to unsigned or 2's complement binary numbers. # pp.646-651

## families
### rns_scaling_comparison  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Each CRT summand is scaled into a binary fraction, and the ordinary binary sum retains one integer bit while multiples of 2 are discarded. The retained leftmost bit directly gives the sign relative to M/2. Truncation precision is bounded so accumulated table errors cannot move a value across the positive/negative boundary. # pp.648-649

### rns_reverse_converter  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Each CRT summand si is represented by quotient qi and remainder ri with respect to M/mj. The quotients are accumulated modulo mj, while the remainders are accumulated by ordinary binary addition. A lookup table forms q(M/2^k) plus a bias, and binary additions recombine the sums and conditionally correct by M using the most significant carry bit. # pp.649-650
