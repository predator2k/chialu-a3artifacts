## summary
The document proposes a second-order piecewise activation function with a tanh-like transition and constant saturation regions. The direct digital realization requires one binary multiplication, one binary shift, and one binary addition, while network simulations show generalisation similar to a bipolar sigmoid and slightly faster convergence.

mechanism: The bipolar activation uses constant outputs of 1 and -1 outside ±L and two second-order pieces inside that interval. The coefficients are selected so the function reaches saturation with zero derivative at ±L. Unity slope at z = 0 gives L = 2.
