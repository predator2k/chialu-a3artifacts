## summary
FPnew is a configurable IEEE 754-oriented floating-point unit with scalar/SIMD arithmetic, mixed-format FMA, division/square root, comparisons, and conversions (pp.2–4). Parallel format-specific slices and merged multi-format slices trade area against pipeline/energy flexibility (p.3). A 22FDX implementation supports five formats and reaches 25.33 Gflop/s and 2.95 Tflop/s W for 8-way FP8 SIMD (p.9).

## families
### multi_precision_simd_fma  (role: extends)
mechanism: The ADDMUL operation group contains fully pipelined single-path FMA units. Parallel slices instantiate format-specific vector lanes, while merged slices share one multi-format datapath. A merged FMA multiplies in `src_fmt` and adds in `dst_fmt`, implementing `dst_fmt fma(src_fmt, src_fmt, dst_fmt)`. Unused lanes and format slices are silenced or clock-gated.

### shift_round_convert  (role: instantiates)

## new_families
### configurable_transprecision_fpu  (domain: fp, closest: multi_precision_simd_fma, why_not: `multi_precision_simd_fma` covers the FMA datapath but not the per-operation-group architecture spanning division/square root, comparison, conversion, routing, arbitration, and control.)
mechanism: The FPU routes up to three operands into independently configurable ADDMUL, DIVSQRT, COMP, and CONV operation groups. Each group may contain disabled, parallel format-specific, or merged multi-format slices. ## space_gaps
* `multi_precision_simd_fma.lane_split` needs to represent several simultaneously supported lane configurations because FPnew supports 1×FP64, 2×FP32, 4×FP16/FP16alt, and 8×FP8 (Table II p.6).
* `multi_precision_simd_fma` lacks the parallel-versus-merged slice choice that controls area, latency flexibility, and energy proportionality (p.3).
* The vocabulary lacks a top-level configurable multi-format FPU family spanning independently configured arithmetic/division/comparison/conversion groups (Fig. 2 pp.3–4).

## open_questions
* The paper does not identify the FMA multiplier, final CPA, alignment, LZA, or rounding subfamilies.
* The exact numerical error after shortening DIVSQRT iteration count is not reported.
* FP16alt has the bfloat16 exponent/fraction widths but retains IEEE denormals/infinities/NaNs and all rounding modes, so the merge pass must not silently normalize it to another bfloat16 contract.
