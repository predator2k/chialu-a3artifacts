## summary
The paper proposes a modulo (2^n + 1) multiplier that combines Booth bit-pair recoding, diminished-1 partial products, a CSA compression tree, and two modulo carry-save adders for residue reduction. The analytical gate-delay model reports multiplier-delay savings of 13% to 14% against Curiger et al. for n from 16 to 64.

### rns_channel_arithmetic  (role: extends)
mechanism: The multiplier operates in a modulo (2^n + 1) channel using diminished-1 representations. Booth-recoded partial products are compressed to sum/carry vectors R0 and R1. ### booth_recoded_parallel  (role: extends)
mechanism: Bit-pair recoding encodes 2(d2i-d2i+1)+(d2i-1-d2i) with Booth’s algorithm. Each partial product is formed in diminished-1 representation using cyclic shifts and complement operations on A-1. ## space_gaps
* `rns_channel_arithmetic.multiplier_reduction` does not distinguish the paper’s fixed two-MCSA diminished-1 residue-reduction structure from generic `csa_with_periodic_folding`. # pp.335-337

## open_questions
* The paper permits a Wallace tree or CSA array, but the reported multiplier-delay analysis assumes a CSA tree built from (3,2) counters.
* The final modulo (2^n + 1) CLA topology is not specified beyond its similarity to a modulo (2^n - 1) CLA.
* The paper reports analytical gate delays rather than fabricated area, power, or measured timing, and the technology node is UNKNOWN.
