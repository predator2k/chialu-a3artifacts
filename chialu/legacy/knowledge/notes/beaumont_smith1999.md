## summary
The paper implements a 3-cycle IEEE-754 fp64 adder that merges significand addition and rounding with a flagged prefix adder. The paper also proposes a 2-cycle accumulation mode and a separate 2-cycle near/far FP adder with an exponent-difference prediction scheme.

## families
### compound_flagged_prefix  (role: extends)
mechanism: A modified parallel-prefix carry-lookahead tree generates flag bits for strings of propagate signals beginning at the second LSB. A preceding half-adder row converts kill/generate patterns into propagate strings, and flagged inversion cells conditionally produce incremented or complemented results. Modified logic at the two least-significant cells adds plus “2” support for rounding after a significand overflow. # p.4

slots:
  none
parameters: 54-bit internal significand inputs; flags begin at the second LSB   # pp.3-4

### single_path  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Stage 1 subtracts exponents, swaps/unpacks operands, and aligns the smaller significand. Stage 2 performs significand addition and all four rounding modes through the flagged prefix adder. Stage 3 uses an LZA-produced distance for normalization and corrects a possible one-bit LZA overestimate or rounded overflow. # pp.2-4

### two_path  (role: proposes)
mechanism: A far path handles all true additions and true subtractions with exponent difference at least 2, plus selected difference-1 cases. A near path speculatively handles cancellation-prone true subtractions with exponent difference 0 or 1. Significand MSBs and exponent LSBs predict which operand needs a one-bit shift, which removes one speculative significand adder and its result multiplexer. # pp.6-7

## new_families
### unnormalized_feedback_fp_accumulator  (domain: fp: floating-point adders, closest: single_path, why_not: the vocabulary has no fixed-latency FP accumulation family that feeds back a rounded but unnormalized result and its normalization distance)
mechanism: The second pipeline stage feeds back the correctly rounded, possibly unnormalized significand with its exponent/sign and predicted normalization distance. The next operation overlaps normalization of the accumulated result with alignment of the new addend. Bypass multiplexers select whether the feedback significand bypasses normalization, bypasses alignment, or passes through both shifts. # pp.5-6

## space_gaps
* `compound_flagged_prefix.outputs` does not cover the paper’s plus “2” and complemented output set. # p.4
* The FP-adder vocabulary lacks feedback of a correctly rounded but unnormalized result with its normalization distance for 2-cycle accumulation. # pp.5-6
* `two_path` lacks a choice for exponent-LSB/significand-MSB prediction of exponent-difference-1 alignment. # pp.6-7

## open_questions
* The paper does not identify the named topology of the flagged prefix carry tree. # p.4
* The paper reports no frequency/cycle time/power for the fabricated 3-cycle chip. # p.5
* The proposed 2-cycle accumulator and 2-cycle near/far adder have no physical implementation results. # pp.5-7
