## summary
The document proposes an Error Tolerant Multiplier that computes the high-order operand parts conventionally and replaces low-order partial products/carry propagation with cascading product-bit logic. A 12-bit CMOS implementation trades numerical accuracy for lower power/delay/area than a conventional 12-bit parallel multiplier.

## families
### error_analysis_quality  (role: analyzes)
mechanism: Overall error OE is the absolute difference between the correct and obtained results. Accuracy ACC expresses relative error as a percentage, minimum acceptance accuracy MAA sets the application-specific threshold, and acceptance probability AP measures the probability that ACC exceeds MAA. Random input patterns evaluate AP for multiplier widths from 4 to 20 bits. # p.1–2

  metric: accuracy, acceptance_probability [outside domain]   # p.1

## new_families
### split_operand_error_tolerant_multiplier  (domain: approx: approximate multipliers, closest: truncated_fixed_width, why_not: ETM emits a full-width approximate result through cascading low-part logic and includes an exact small-operand fallback rather than only dropping low product columns with compensation.)
mechanism: Each operand is split into a high-order multiplication part and a low-order non-multiplication part. A conventional parallel multiplier computes the high product bits. Cascading POC cells scan the low operand bits from MSB to LSB; the first position containing a 1 sets that and all lower product bits to 1, while preceding 00 positions produce 0. The low part generates no partial products and has no carry-propagation path. A control block selects a conventional lower-width multiplier when both high operand halves contain no 1.   # p.2–3
choices:
  split_width_bits: Int[1..n-1:1] — the 12-bit instance uses two 6-bit parts   # p.2–3
  lower_result_generation: {cascading_poc} — POC cells synthesize low product bits without partial products   # p.2–3
  small_operand_fallback: Bool — the implemented control/MUX selects a conventional 6-bit multiplier   # p.3
  upper_multiplier_style: {conventional_parallel} — a 6-bit parallel multiplier produces P12–P23   # p.3

* error_analysis_quality.metric lacks OE/ACC/AP and the associated MAA threshold. # p.1

## open_questions

* Fig. 2 gives AP curves without tabulated values, so exact AP values must remain UNKNOWN.
