## summary
The document develops modular serial pipeline multipliers for two’s complement data and sign-and-magnitude or two’s complement coefficients. The document compares direct sign handling, Booth recoding, five-level recoding, and custom fixed-coefficient recoding, including stage complexity and rounding/overflow behavior.

## families
### serial_serial_parallel  (role: extends)
mechanism: An N-bit data word enters least-significant bit first while coefficient-controlled modules accumulate partial products from least to most significant. Each module truncates the partial-product sum before the next addition, and inserted registers permit a new operation every N bit times despite an N + K bit-time operation delay. Sign-extension logic permits direct two’s complement partial-product addition. Coefficient/control registers can make all non-power connections serial. Sign-and-magnitude, direct two’s complement, Booth ternary, five-level, and fixed-coefficient variants change the final stages or module recoding. (pp.419-424)

errors_and_checks: Truncating two’s complement partial-product sums implements a floor function in the designs with two’s complement coefficients; the sign-and-magnitude coefficient design can produce different truncations after its final true/complement operation. Adding one LSB-valued offset implements nearest-integer rounding, with half integers rounding up in two’s complement designs but either up or down after sign-and-magnitude complementation. A positive offset can cause internal overflow in the Booth design when the first recoded digit is -1, and the one-all-zeros input code causes another Booth overflow case; suppressing the first sign extension and supplying a correct data-word sign extension cures both. The five-level design lacks these stated problems.   # pp.422-424, Fig. 10

## space_gaps
* serial_serial_parallel lacks a choice for overlapped word pipelining, which separates the N-bit-time initiation interval from the N + K bit-time operation delay.   # p.419
* serial_serial_parallel lacks coefficient representation/recoding choices for sign-and-magnitude, two’s complement, Booth ternary, five-level, and fixed custom coefficients.   # pp.420-424
* serial_serial_parallel lacks a coefficient-delivery choice covering parallel inputs, serial coefficient registers, and hardwired fixed coefficients.   # pp.419-422
* serial_serial_parallel lacks an explicit rounding-offset/sign-extension choice, although those mechanisms determine rounding direction and internal overflow.   # pp.419, 422-424
