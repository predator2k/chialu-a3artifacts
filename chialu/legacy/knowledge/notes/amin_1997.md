## summary
The paper proposes PLAN, a piecewise-linear sigmoid approximation whose power-of-two slopes permit shift/add interpolation and then a combinational direct transformation from input bits to output bits. The implementation uses symmetry, magnitude-range flags, direct bit mapping, and parallel generation of Y and 1-Y to obtain the activation output in 11 gate delays after two's complementation. Neural-network simulations compare PLAN with the exact sigmoid, A-law, and two quadratic-equation variants.

## families
### sigmoid_tanh_pwl  (role: proposes)
mechanism: PLAN folds the sigmoid around Y=0.5, classifies |X| into four ranges, and evaluates straight-line approximations with slopes 0.25, 0.125, 0.03125, and 0. The slopes permit shifts instead of multipliers. DTXY replaces the remaining shift/add interpolation with fixed Boolean mappings from X-register bits and range flags to Y-register bits. A parallel DTXY path produces 1-Y from the two's-complemented input, and the sign selects Y or 1-Y. # pp.314-316

conditions: PLAN removes multipliers because every nonzero slope is a negative power of two. DTXY additionally depends on fixed register-bit positions and fixed breakpoints. PLAN's gradient is slightly below the sigmoid gradient for inputs from 2.375 to 5 and from -2.375 to -5, which slightly increases convergence time. Saturation occurs beyond ±5, and the derivative floor E=0.005 prevents inactive neurons near local minima.   # p.314

### pwl  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The sigmoid is divided into nonuniform intervals whose linear coefficients are selected so multiplication reduces to right shifts. The positive-domain equations are Y=0.25|X|+0.5, Y=0.125|X|+0.625, Y=0.03125|X|+0.84375, and Y=1. Symmetry supplies the negative-domain result. The proposed hardware maps input bits directly to output bits instead of executing the equations. # pp.314-316

## space_gaps

## open_questions
* The exact input/output word widths are not stated; Fig. 9 identifies sign bit 8 and decimal points after X(4) and Sig Y(7).   # p.316
* The paper describes four |X| ranges, which correspond to eight full-domain segments when mirrored; the intended segment-count convention is not stated.   # p.314
* No fabrication technology, synthesized area, physical clock rate, power, or numerical approximation-error metric is reported.   # pp.313-317
