## summary
The paper derives three residue-to-binary converters for the moduli set (2^n-1, 2^n, 2^n+1) from New CRT-I. Converter I uses a 2n-bit adder, while Converters II and III use n-bit adders and trade hardware for delay (pp.1776–1778).

## families
### rns_reverse_converter  (role: proposes)
mechanism: New CRT-I reduces conversion for the three-modulus set to additions of rearranged/complemented residue fields. Converter I combines intermediate carry-save values with a 2n-bit 1's-complement adder and concatenates the remaining residue field. Converter II uses four parallel n-bit carry-lookahead adders and carry-dependent selection. Converter III replaces two of those adders with combinational increment-by-one circuits, which reduces delay at increased hardware cost relative to Converter II (pp.1773–1778).
