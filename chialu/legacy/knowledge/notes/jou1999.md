## summary
The paper proposes fixed-width sign-magnitude/two’s-complement parallel multipliers that retain n product bits from two n-bit operands and use data-dependent carry-generating circuits to reduce truncation error (pp.836-840). The method also produces 2n-to-m reduced-width multipliers for n < m < 2n (p.840).

## families
### truncated_fixed_width  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The multiplier removes the partial-product cells that generate discarded low product bits and feeds revised carry information into the retained array. For sign-magnitude multiplication, Cg uses the boundary products xi yj with i+j=n-1 and comprises n-2 AO cells plus one two-input AND gate. For two’s-complement multiplication, Cg comprises n-2 OR cells plus one two-input NOR gate. The reduced-width form removes the cells for the 2n-m least-significant bits and uses a 2n-m-bit Cg. (pp.837-840)
