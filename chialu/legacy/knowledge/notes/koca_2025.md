## summary
The document proposes standard-variance and pairwise-variance Layer Normalization accelerators using 16-bit fixed-point arithmetic and nonuniform PWL approximations for square root and reciprocal functions (p.2-p.3). The pairwise design reduces processing latency from 52 to 38 cycles, while the approximation causes average accuracy drops of 0.06% for BERT and 0.28% for DistilBERT without fine tuning (p.4).

## families
### softmax_layernorm  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The standard architecture processes each 512-element vector in three data-dependent passes and uses seven pipeline stages for mean, squaring, variance, square root/reciprocal, normalization, scaling and shifting. The pairwise architecture calculates group means and variances separately, combines them through pairwise variance equations, and fully pipelines the first three stages. The pairwise schedule reduces the input passes to two and removes the post-adder-tree accumulators, while stages s4-s7 remain unchanged. (p.3)

errors_and_checks: The reported accuracy contract is empirical task accuracy rather than a numeric arithmetic-error bound; the maximum reported task-accuracy drop is 1.09%, and Python-generated golden vectors provide functional verification (p.4).
conditions: The standard architecture cannot fully pipeline s1-s3 because squaring waits until the global mean is complete (p.3). The pairwise algorithm requires O(log(n)) intermediate memory rather than the standard algorithm’s O(1), and larger group counts increase hardware parallelism and scheduling complexity (p.3). Both proposed designs target pretrained BERT/DistilBERT inference without fine tuning (p.1, p.4).
evidence: §III-A-D, Fig. 2, Fig. 4, Fig. 5, Table I, Table III and Table IV (p.2-p.4).

### pwl  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Seven nonuniform breakpoints divide each nonlinear function into eight line segments. Seven parallel subtractors compare Idata with the breakpoints, and their sign flags feed a priority encoder that selects a stored gradient/intercept pair. One multiplier and one adder evaluate the selected line. The same circuit approximates square root or reciprocal by changing the stored coefficients. A half-round-up operation produces the 16-bit result after the truncated fixed-width addition. (p.2-p.3)

## space_gaps
* softmax_layernorm lacks a slot for the nonlinear square-root/reciprocal evaluator, which this design fills with pwl (p.2-p.3).
* softmax_layernorm lacks variance_algorithm and degree_of_parallelism choices needed to distinguish the standard and pairwise architectures (p.2-p.3).
* pwl lacks an output_rounding choice for the reported half-round-up treatment of its truncated fixed-width result (p.3).

## open_questions
* The document does not report the stored gradient/intercept word lengths or coefficient fractional widths.
* The document does not report maximum ulp/absolute/relative errors for the PWL square-root and reciprocal functions.
* Table I reports one accuracy result for both architectures because the standard and pairwise calculations are stated to be numerically equal, so architecture-specific accuracy is unavailable.
