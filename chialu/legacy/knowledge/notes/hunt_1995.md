## summary
The PA-8000 contains two fully pipelined floating-point multiply-and-accumulate units that compute D = A*B+C and also execute standalone multiply/add operations (p.127). The processor also contains two nonpipelined floating-point divide/square-root units with separate single-precision and double-precision latencies, but the document does not disclose their arithmetic recurrence (p.127).

## space_gaps
* The vocabulary lacks a mechanism-neutral family for a commercial black-box combined floating-point divide/square-root unit whose recurrence is undisclosed; the PA-8000 provides two such nonpipelined units with latencies of 17 cycles for single precision and 31 cycles for double precision (p.127).

## open_questions
* The document does not state whether D = A*B+C uses one rounding or separate multiply/add rounding, so fused-rounding semantics must not be assumed.
* The document does not identify the divide/square-root algorithm, radix, digit set, internal representation, sharing structure, or rounding method.
* The document states that each integer unit includes shift/merge logic for extract/deposit instructions, but it does not disclose enough circuitry to assign a shifter family (p.127).
