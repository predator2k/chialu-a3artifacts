## summary
The paper proposes a fixed-width modified Booth multiplier whose compensation bias is generated from Booth encoder outputs. The method partitions discarded partial products into major/minor groups and estimates their carry with exhaustive or statistical approximate-carry generation. Simulations report lower quantization error than [8] and about 35% area/power savings against an ideal multiplier.

### truncated_fixed_width  (role: extends)
mechanism: The architecture omits the adder cells for the discarded LP region and injects an estimated carry into MP. LP is divided into major/minor groups according to each group's effect on quantization error. The proposed bias combines an exact carry from the major group with an approximate carry derived from Booth encoder outputs for the minor group. ACGP I derives Boolean carry signals by statistical thresholds/Karnaugh maps. ACGP II replaces the growing maps with a tree of full/half adders and a final increment.
