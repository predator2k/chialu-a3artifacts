## summary
The paper describes the application-driven co-design of the A64FX processor and Fugaku system. A64FX provides two 512-bit SVE arithmetic units per core, supports half/single/double precision, and uses an FMA arithmetic unit for many floating-point operations. The paper also establishes selectable power modes that trade frequency and active arithmetic pipelines against workload performance and power.

## families
### multi_precision_simd_fma  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Each core contains two 512-bit SVE SIMD arithmetic units (p.4). Many floating-point operations execute in nine cycles using an FMA arithmetic unit, which reduces die size while increasing instruction latency (p.11). The chip supplies DP/SP/HP arithmetic in normal and boost modes (p.10).

## new_families
### arithmetic_pipeline_power_modes  (domain: fp, closest: low_power_gated, why_not: low_power_gated partitions floating-point-adder paths by operand case, while A64FX selects frequency and the number of active arithmetic pipelines by software-controlled operating mode)
mechanism: A64FX exposes normal, boost, normal+eco, and boost+eco modes (p.8). Boost mode raises frequency from 2.0 GHz to 2.2 GHz (p.8). Eco mode leaves only one arithmetic unit active and applies a stable-operation mechanism to the inactive unit, allowing memory-intensive workloads to reduce power without reducing arithmetic throughput that the workload does not use (pp.7-8).

## space_gaps
* multi_precision_simd_fma lacks choices for SIMD width, arithmetic-pipeline count, and shared-operation latency, which A64FX fixes at 512 bits, two pipelines/core, and nine cycles (pp.4, 11).
* The vocabulary lacks a family for software-selectable arithmetic-pipeline power modes that combine frequency selection with execution-unit deactivation (pp.7-8).

## open_questions
* The paper does not state whether DP/SP/HP operations share the same physical lane datapath, so lane_split is UNKNOWN (pp.4, 10).
* The paper does not describe the multiplier, final CPA, rounding, or subnormal-handling structures inside the FMA arithmetic unit (p.11).
* Table VII does not identify a technology node or result year for its modeled power-knob values, so both remain UNKNOWN (p.7).
