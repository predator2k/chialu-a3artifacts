## summary
The document proposes tensor-wise variance scaling and warmup training for DNN training with posit(8,1), while retaining posit(16,1) for the last layer and weight master copy. It implements GEMM as a 16x16 systolic array whose posit operands feed fused multiply-add units with float(1,8,23) accumulation. The synthesized array reduces area, power, and external memory width relative to an FP32 array. 

## families
### posit_adder_multiplier  (role: extends)
mechanism: Posit(8,1) weights and activations are decoded into float(1,8,4), multiplied exactly at their effective precision, and accumulated in float(1,8,23). The decoder detects leading zeros and ones concurrently, overlaps regime detection with a preshift, and bypasses negative-input two’s-complement conversion by separating the sign. The encoder applies the corresponding sign-separated conversion. The training framework uses variance-based tensor scaling, round-to-nearest quantization, posit(8,1) for most tensors, and posit(16,1) for the last layer and weight master copy.

errors_and_checks: Quantization uses round-to-nearest and reports empirical task accuracy rather than a formal arithmetic error bound. Posit results remain within 0.33% top-1 degradation across the reported image models, while Penn Treebank perplexity changes from 61.6825 to 61.7072. The FMA product is exact for the decoded posit multiplicands, and its output is kept consistent with the GPU simulation.   # p.6, p.9, p.11
conditions: Posit(8,0) is rejected because its mean error is no better than float(8,5), while posit(8,1) has lower reported quantization error than posit(8,2). Variance scaling is preferred over log-mean scaling because sparse batch-normalization gradients deviate from a normal distribution. ResNet18 loses 1.3% accuracy when the last-layer activations become 8-bit and loses 1.9% when all last-layer computations use posit8. AlexNet loses 2.3% when only loss scaling is used. Warmup training is needed when a warmup learning-rate schedule causes early distribution changes.   # p.7, p.8, p.9, p.10

## new_families
### posit_systolic_mixed_format_fma  (domain: dsp: posit units, closest: posit_quire_mac, why_not: the accumulator is float(1,8,23) rather than an exact posit quire, and the architecture exposes encoder/decoder placement across a systolic array)
mechanism: Each processing element receives posit(8,1) weights and activations, decodes them to float(1,8,4), and computes A × B + C with a float(1,8,23) accumulated operand. Weights move horizontally, activations move vertically, and results move vertically. Encoders and decoders can reside in every processing element or be shared at array boundaries. Boundary sharing reduces codec count but changes register width and sequential storage requirements.

## space_gaps
* `posit_adder_multiplier` lacks fused accumulation format and encoder/decoder placement choices needed by this design.   # p.10, p.12
* The posit-unit vocabulary lacks a mixed-format systolic FMA whose posit products accumulate in conventional floating-point rather than a quire.   # p.10-13

## open_questions
