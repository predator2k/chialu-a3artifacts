## summary
The document presents a decimal64 floating-point adder that combines parallel operand alignment, decimal injection-based rounding, and a flagged Kogge-Stone network (§3). The document extends the adder into an eight-operation multifunction unit and reports synthesis comparisons against an earlier decimal floating-point adder (§4, §5).

## space_gaps
* `decimal_fp_addition` lacks an `operation_set` choice for adder-based multifunction units that also implement compare/minNum/maxNum/quantize/sameQuantum/roundToIntegral (§4).
