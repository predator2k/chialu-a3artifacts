## summary
The chapter defines Karatsuba-Ofman decompositions, target-aware DSP tilings, squarers, and faithfully rounded truncated multipliers for FPGA implementation. The chapter settles DSP/logic/latency trade-offs for single-precision, double-precision, and quadruple-precision significand widths on Virtex-4/5/6 and Stratix-II/III/IV devices. The chapter reports placed-and-routed results obtained with ISE 11.5 / LogiCore Multiplier 11 unless stated otherwise.   # p.81

## families
### recursive_karatsuba  (role: extends)
mechanism: Operands are split into k-bit subwords. Diagonal products XiYi are computed directly, while each symmetric cross-product pair XiYj + XjYi is recovered as Pii + Pjj - (Xi-Xj)(Yi-Yj). An N-part direct expansion therefore uses N(N+1)/2 embedded multipliers. Two-way recursive splitting uses three half-size products instead of four, but the direct N-part form better matches DSP adders and regular FPGA pipelines.   # p.83, p.87-p.89
choices:
  recursion_depth: 1, 2   # p.83, p.86
  split_kind: two_way   # p.83
  base_multiplier: fpga_dsp_block   # p.83-p.89
new_choices:
  partition_count: Int[2..15:1] — number of k-bit operand parts in the direct decomposition   # p.84-p.89
  difference_form: subtractive — precompute Xi-Xj and Yi-Yj rather than sums   # p.83
slots:
  none

### squarer  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Symmetry removes duplicate off-diagonal products. A two-part square uses X1², X0², and X1X0, while a three-part square uses three sub-squares and three distinct cross-products. Device-specific arrangements shift an operand before a DSP, reuse independently controlled DSP submultipliers, or tile symmetrically and subtract diagonal overlaps.   # p.93-p.95

### truncated_fixed_width  (role: extends)
mechanism: Low-weight multiplication-array columns are omitted, and an offset correction centers the truncation error. Round-to-nearest adds a bit at position 2^(k-1), while a second correction at that position allocates half the 2^k faithful-error budget to truncation. FPGA tiling counts full DSP outputs that extend beyond the virtual truncation line as free compensation, then adds soft multipliers until the error bound is met.   # p.95-p.98
choices:
  extra_columns_kept: UNKNOWN   # p.95-p.98
  correction_scheme: constant   # p.96
  output_rounding: round_to_nearest   # p.96
new_choices:
  discarded_columns: d — number of omitted least-significant multiplication-array columns   # p.95-p.96
  compensation_source: {offset_bit, free_dsp_bits, soft_multiplier_tiles} — computed terms used to meet the faithful-error bound   # p.96-p.98

## new_families
### target_aware_dsp_tiling  (domain: mul: integer multipliers, closest: segmented_grid, why_not: segmented_grid fixes regular 16-bit segmentation, while this mechanism searches arbitrary rectangular tiles and device-specific DSP super-tiles)
mechanism: The multiplication board is partitioned into non-overlapping rectangular products. Compatible products are grouped into super-tiles whose shifts/cascade connections match a target DSP block, which reduces final-adder operands. A cost search selects DSP tiles, logic tiles, and a multioperand adder according to an explicit DSP-to-logic ratio. Symmetry, aligned edges, and hole rejection prune the exponential configuration space.   # p.90-p.92
choices: dsp_logic_ratio: Real[0..1]; tile_partition: aligned_rectangles; super_tile_grouping: target_primitives; target: {Virtex4, Virtex5, Virtex6, StratixII, StratixIII, StratixIV}; search_pruning: {symmetry, aligned_edges, reject_large_holes}   # p.91-p.92

## space_gaps
* recursive_karatsuba needs a partition_count choice because the chapter directly implements 3/4/5/6/7/15-part decompositions beyond the two-way recursion vocabulary.   # p.84-p.89
* The multiplier vocabulary lacks target-aware arbitrary DSP/logic tiling and DSP super-tile grouping.   # p.90-p.93
* squarer lacks a device_mapping choice for DSP-specific symmetry implementations.   # p.94-p.95
* truncated_fixed_width lacks discarded_columns and free-DSP-bit compensation choices.   # p.95-p.98

## open_questions

* The chapter does not specify the reduction-tree or final-adder family used by the squarer and truncated-multiplier implementations.   # p.93-p.98
