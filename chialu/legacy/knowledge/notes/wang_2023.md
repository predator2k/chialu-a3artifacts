## summary
SOLE co-designs E2Softmax and AILayerNorm to reduce arithmetic precision and intermediate-storage width for transformer inference. E2Softmax combines 4-bit log2-quantized exponent outputs with shift/add exponent evaluation and approximate log-based division, while AILayerNorm dynamically compresses 8-bit inputs to 4 bits for statistic calculation. The synthesized units improve speed/energy-efficiency/area-efficiency while maintaining evaluated model accuracy without retraining.

## families
### softmax_layernorm  (role: proposes)
mechanism: E2Softmax subtracts the online maximum, maps exponent outputs to 4-bit log2 codes with a shift/add Log2Exp unit, reduces the codes with online normalization, and normalizes through a Mitchell-style divider using a leading-one detector, subtraction, multiplexing, and shifts. AILayerNorm dynamically compresses 8-bit inputs to 4 bits for squared-input accumulation, restores scale with shifts, computes mean/inverse standard deviation, and applies an 8-bit affine transform. Both units use two-stage pipelines and ping-pong buffers.

errors_and_checks: Dynamic compression produces errors of 0.2% over E(x²) and 0.4% over standard deviation for uniformly distributed inputs (p.4). Evaluated inference has a worst accuracy drop under 0.9% and an average drop nearly 0.38% against FP32; against INT8, the worst drop is 0.8% and the average drop is 0.2% (p.7). No retraining or fine-tuning is required for SOLE (p.7).
conditions: E2Softmax relies on inputs shifted by the vector maximum so Log2Exp receives values in (-∞, 0] (p.3). AILayerNorm assumes squared-input reduction is less sensitive to small input values and uses PTF-quantized 8-bit activations (p.4). The units support batch size 1 and integrate with 8-bit vector MAC/tensor-core datapaths through 8-bit inputs and outputs (p.6-p.7). Memory savings depend on reducing Softmax/LayerNorm buffer widths from 16/32 bits to 4/8 bits (p.8).
evidence: Algorithms 1-2 and Equations 7-17 (p.3-p.5); Figures 4-5 (p.5-p.6); Tables I-III and Figure 6 (p.7-p.8).

## space_gaps
* softmax_layernorm lacks choices for exponent-output quantization/bit width, online normalization, LayerNorm statistic compression, and intermediate-buffer width, which distinguish E2Softmax and AILayerNorm from other implementations (p.3-p.8).
* softmax_layernorm lacks a slot or choice for the LayerNorm square/inverse-standard-deviation implementation, including the 16-entry square LUT and small x^-0.5 LUT used here (p.6).

## open_questions
* The paper reports efficiency ratios but does not report absolute area, power, energy, latency, or throughput for SOLE or the reimplemented custom-hardware baselines (p.7-p.8).
* The paper does not specify the precision/implementation of every Stage 2 affine multiplier or the complete numeric accuracy bound of the approximate reciprocal-square-root path (p.6).
