## summary
The paper proposes Mitch-w, a configurable Mitchell logarithmic multiplier that truncates operand mantissas and customizes the antilogarithm hardware for CNN inference (pp.662-663). The paper also proposes one’s-complement signed handling and error-unbiased variants, then evaluates their hardware cost/error and CNN classification accuracy (pp.664-674).

## families
### logarithmic  (role: extends)
mechanism: A fully parallel one-hot LOD and OR-tree encoder obtain each operand’s characteristic, while left shifts expose the w-1 retained mantissa bits (pp.662-663). The characteristic/mantissa operands are added in the approximate logarithmic domain, and a customized antilogarithm uses an n+w left shifter plus w-bit right-shifter/multiplexer hardware (p.663). Exact zero detection overrides the approximate output (p.663). The signed variant XORs inputs with their sign bits to approximate magnitude conversion by C1 and XORs a negative result back into signed form (pp.664-665).

errors_and_checks: Truncation adds relative error below 2^-w+2 with respect to the conventional Mitchell multiplier (p.664). For N=32 and w=8, the original variant reports mean error -4.4%, PWCE 0%, and NWCE -12.0%; the unbiased variant reports mean error 0.4%, PWCE 7.7%, and NWCE -8.2% (p.668). C1 signed handling has high relative-error corner cases for -1 and -2, but their absolute errors remain small (p.667).
conditions: The optimized shift calculations assume n is a power of two; some optimizations do not apply otherwise (p.662). Smaller w reduces area/energy but increases the truncation-error bound, so the appropriate w depends on the application/network/dataset (pp.664,671). Approximate multiplication is used for inference rather than training because its error prevented backpropagation convergence (p.668). C1 handling is supported for CNN MACs because multiplication errors accumulate in absolute magnitude; the paper warns that high relative error may be unacceptable for multiplication chains (p.672). CNN accuracy depends more on error range than mean error because differing accumulated errors can reverse output-score order (pp.670-671).
evidence: Algorithm 1 and Figs. 1-5 (pp.662-665); error analyses in §§4.1, 5.1, and 6 (pp.663-668); synthesis Tables 2-4 and 13 (pp.668-669,673); CNN Tables 6-15 (pp.669-673).

## space_gaps
* The logarithmic family needs an operand_truncation_width choice for Mitch-w-style retained mantissa precision (pp.662-664).
* The logarithmic family needs signed_number_handling values for C2 and C1_approx conversion (pp.664-667).
* The logarithmic correction domain needs constant_mantissa_shift for the paper’s 0.0001 unbiasing constant (p.667).
* The logarithmic family needs an exact_zero_detection choice because zero handling materially affects CNN accuracy and hardware structure (pp.663,672).
