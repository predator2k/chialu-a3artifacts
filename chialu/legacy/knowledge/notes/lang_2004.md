## summary
The paper proposes a double-precision floating-point multiply-add-fused architecture that performs normalization before a combined addition/rounding stage and overlaps the LZA with normalization shifting (pp. 988, 991-992). The architecture anticipates part of a dual prefix adder and has an estimated delay reduction of between 15 percent and 20 percent relative to the basic MAF architecture (pp. 1001-1002).

## families
### reduced_latency_fma  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The architecture aligns A in parallel with multiplication, merges the aligned addend and carry-save product through a 3:2 CSA, predicts the normalization distance with an LZA, and normalizes before final carry propagation. The LZA emits the shift encoding from its most-significant bit so normalization can begin before the complete amount is known. A 53-bit dual adder computes sum and sum+1 for combined addition and rounding. Generate/propagate/kill calculation and two prefix levels are anticipated before normalization, with shifted generate/propagate vectors corrected afterward. Sign detection controls conditional complementation for effective subtraction (pp. 991-1002).

### classic_fma  (role: compares)
mechanism: The basic MAF aligns and optionally complements A in parallel with multiplication, combines A with the carry-save product in a 3:2 CSA, resolves a 161-bit one’s-complement sum with end-around-carry adjustment, normalizes after addition, and rounds in a separate final stage. Its implementation divides carry propagation into a 106-bit adder and a 53-bit incrementer selected by the lower carry (pp.989-992, 1001).

## space_gaps
* `reduced_latency_fma` lacks choices for MSB-first LZA/normalizer overlap and the number of prefix levels anticipated before normalization (pp.996-997, 1002).
* `reduced_latency_fma` lacks a sign/complement-handling choice for the conditional two’s-complement treatment used around the CSA and normalization stages (pp.992, 994-996).
* The `round` slot vocabulary lacks an explicit dual-adder family definition even though `compound_adder_select` names the mechanism used by this design (pp.992, 998-999).

## open_questions
* The proposed dual adder’s prefix topology is unspecified; Fig. 8 uses Kogge-Stone only as an example and states that another prefix adder could be used (p.999).
* The fabrication technology and device are not reported because the evaluation uses technology-independent `tinv4` estimates (pp.1001-1002).
* The pipeline depth, cycle time, and initiation interval are not fixed because their improvement depends on implementation constraints (p.1002).
