## summary
The document proposes an IEEE 754-2008/ARM-compliant FMA microarchitecture that composes separate early-normalizing multiply and add pipelines, which allows the augend to arrive after multiplication begins (pp.123-124, 127). The multiplier shifts partial products before injection rounding, while the unified-path adder performs LZA-driven prenormalization before its final addition (pp.125-127). A modeled 4+4-stage implementation outperforms a traditional 7-stage fused pipeline on six Cortex-A8/NEON benchmarks (pp.124-125).

## families
### bridge_fma  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The FMA begins multiplication without the augend and consumes the augend when the add pipeline begins. The multiplier forwards an unrounded 106-bit mantissa plus exponent/status information into the separate adder. Dependent FMAs can therefore begin according to add latency rather than full fused-pipeline latency (pp.124-125, 127).

### delay_optimized_unified  (role: extends)
mechanism: A four-stage unified add path computes operand ordering and two LZAs in E2. E3 right-aligns ordinary far-path cases or left-shifts cancellation cases according to the LZA, so E4 can add and inject rounding without a post-add normalization cycle. Two final adders absorb LZA correction and support a double-length fused operand (pp.126-127).

### round_fused_in_reduction  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The optional E4N stage shifts the two reduced partial products left or right until their sum is normalized or denormalized at the fixed injection locations. E4 then performs addition and injection rounding in one cycle. For subnormal right shifts, a 62-bit low adder preserves shifted-out carry/sticky information and selects between two main-adder results (pp.125-126).

## space_gaps
* bridge_fma lacks a choice for deferred augend availability, which is essential to the reported dependent-FMA scheduling advantage (pp.124, 127).
* delay_optimized_unified lacks choices for pre-add LZA timing and support for an unrounded double-length fused operand (pp.126-127).
* round_fused_in_reduction lacks a choice for bidirectional partial-product normalization before fixed-location injection rounding (pp.125-126).

## open_questions
