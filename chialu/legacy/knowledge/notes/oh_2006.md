## summary
The paper describes the shipped 4-way SIMD single-precision floating-point unit of the CELL SPE, which executes a multiply-add per 32-bit slice in six cycles at 11 FO4 per stage. IEEE correctness is traded for latency and simple design: truncation rounding only, non-trapping exceptions, denormals flushed to zero, maximum-exponent values treated as regular numbers, and overflow saturating. The unit has 768K transistors in 1.3 mm2 of 90-nm SOI and ran to 5.6 GHz at 1.4 V and 56 C, delivering 44.8 GFlops.

## families
### classic_fma  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The addend aligns while the 25-bit radix-4 Booth multiplier runs, and a 3:2 counter merges the product's sum and carry vectors with the 97-bit aligned addend. The fraction adder is sign-magnitude, split into a 25-bit incrementer, a main adder giving S and S+1, and sticky logic, with a one's complement end-around carry on effective subtraction. The LZA reads the merging counter's output, four shift stages normalize, and the result multiplexer truncates. An extra format stage pre-processes integer and convert operands.

### booth_recoded_parallel  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Radix-4 Booth recoding scans 3 bits with 1-bit overlap of the B-operand, giving rows 0, A, -A, 2A and -2A, and hot-1 encoding covers the negative rows' sign extension. Two's complement recoding plus shifting generates the rows, the negation applied before shifting because the double and single signals are slower than the sign signal. Embedding makes the top row non-negative, so 13 rather than 14 rows enter five static-full-adder stages.

### compound_flagged_prefix  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The sign-magnitude adder always yields a positive result: equal signs add plainly, different signs take a one's complement end-around carry with c the carry-out of A + not(B). Adding c back is stated to be too slow, so the main adder gives S and S+1 and the 25-bit incrementer X and X+1, re-complemented on a subtraction. Instruction type, aligner shift amount, sticky bit and carry-out select among them in a six-port type-C multiplexer latch.

### lza  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: G (NAND), P (XOR) and K (NOR) per bit on the merging 3:2 counter's output detect edges from the current and the two preceding bit positions, so the input sign bit is extended. The first stage emits an edge vector and the second counts its leading zeros. The count is exact or one too large, so a mask vector with one bit set at the shift position is ANDed bit by bit with the adder output into a zero checker.

### barrel_mux_tree  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Aligner and normalizer share 4:1 transmission-gate multiplexer stages, whose inverter drivers beat AOI multiplexers on the long horizontal wires, and the selects are hot-1 over 4 bits. The sum-addressed scheme adds and decodes together: a 4:2 compressor leaves the shift amount in carry-save form, 2-bit sum-decoders emit the selects for the carry and no-carry cases, which differ by a 1-bit rotation, and a block carry generation picks between them.

## open_questions
* Tables I and II and Figs. 1 to 13 are images, so latch-type numbers and the Booth digit table are unrecorded.
* Symbol names and formulas are dropped from the extracted Sections III-D, III-F and III-G, so the six-port latch's selected signals and the pre-computed exponent copies are not recoverable.
* Whether hot-1 encoding forms a negative row as one's complement plus a hot one or as a true two's complement row is unsettled, since the text names both.
* The stage count of the aligner, adder and rounder is not stated, only the multiplier's two cycles and the LZA's two stages.
