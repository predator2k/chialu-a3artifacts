## summary
The paper introduces Mixed-Hybrid and Partitioned-Hybrid CORDIC algorithms for circular-coordinate rotation. The algorithms derive about 2/3 of the rotation directions in parallel without error and support cascaded architectures that trade circuit complexity for latency/throughput.

## families
### cordic  (role: extends)
mechanism: The Mixed-Hybrid algorithm uses circular arctangent radices for the most-significant iterations and radix-2 linear arctangent radices for the least-significant iterations. The least-significant rotation directions become angle bits that are generated in parallel after the sequential prefix completes. The Partitioned-Hybrid algorithm splits q into qH and qL, performs qH as one exact rotation selected by a lookup table including the scale factor, and then applies the radix-2 rotations for qL. Both architectures cascade one subprocessor for each radix subset.
