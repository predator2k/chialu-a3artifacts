## summary
The paper proposes two iterative fixed-point BCD multipliers that keep the partial product in decimal carry-save form. The improved multiplier generates 2A/4A/5A without stored multiples, uses decimal (3:2)/(4:2) compression, and completes an n-digit multiplication with worst-case latency n + 4 cycles and initiation interval n + 1 cycles. # p.1, p.8-p.9

## families
### iterative_decimal_multiplication  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The initial design generates and stores 2A/3A/4A/8A, then applies two serial decimal (3:2) counters per multiplier digit. The improved design generates 2A/4A/5A with dedicated logic, forms each selected primary multiple through a decimal (3:2) counter, and combines that redundant result with the carry-save partial product through a decimal (4:2) compressor. One product digit is retired per iteration, followed by a two-cycle final carry-propagate conversion. # p.5-p.9

### decimal_multioperand_addition  (role: extends)
mechanism: A direct-decimal-addition cell acts as a decimal (3:2) counter when it accepts two BCD digits and one carry bit. The improved multiplier first uses a simplified counter with carry-in fixed to zero. Its feedback stage uses a decimal (4:2) compressor formed from a standard decimal (3:2) counter followed by a simplified decimal (3:2) counter, reducing two BCD sums and two carry bits to one BCD sum and one carry bit. # p.3, p.6-p.8

### bcd_direct_addition  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Direct decimal logic accepts two 4-bit BCD digits and a carry-in and produces a BCD sum digit and decimal carry-out without performing a separate binary sum followed by +6 correction. The same functional equations implement a carry-propagate digit cell or a decimal (3:2) counter. The improved multiplier uses a two-part simplified decimal carry-propagate adder to assimilate the final carry-save product. # p.3, p.8

## space_gaps
* The `iterative_decimal_multiplication.accumulator` slot lacks a decimal carry-save/compressor filler, while the implemented accumulator uses decimal (3:2)/(4:2) compression. # p.5-p.8
* The `iterative_decimal_multiplication.final_adder` and `decimal_multioperand_addition.root_adder` slots exclude decimal-adder families, while the final redundant product is converted by a simplified decimal carry-propagate adder. # p.8
* `compressor_arity` needs a multi-value representation because the improved design uses both decimal 3-to-2 and 4-to-2 primitives. # p.6-p.8
* `iterative_decimal_multiplication` lacks choices for generated-versus-stored multiples, zero skipping, data-dependent two-digit consumption, and early exit. # p.5, p.7, p.9-p.10

## open_questions
* The paper does not report technology, area, frequency, power, or measured average latency.
* The paper gives 10%/20%/25% optimization opportunities but does not translate them into average cycle counts. # p.9-p.10
* The paper states that the fixed-point designs can extend to decimal floating-point multiplication but does not specify the extension hardware or latency. # p.1
