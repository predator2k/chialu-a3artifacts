## summary
The document reports IBM Telum, a 7nm processor with one shared on-chip AI accelerator containing high-density multiply-accumulate systolic arrays and specialized activation blocks. The accelerator provides 6TFLOPS of FP16 compute for matrix multiplication/convolution and supports functions including sigmoid/softmax. # p.46
