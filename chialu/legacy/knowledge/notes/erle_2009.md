## summary
The paper extends iterative decimal-CSA and parallel binary-CSA fixed-point multipliers into IEEE 754-2008 decimal64 floating-point multipliers. # p.903
The extensions provide exponent generation, intermediate-product shifting, sticky-bit generation, rounding, and exception handling. # pp.903-904
The iterative design favors area, while the pipelined parallel design favors latency and throughput. # pp.914-915

## families
### iterative_decimal_multiplication  (role: extends)
mechanism: Each BCD-8421 multiplier digit selects two values from the reduced multiplicand-multiple set {1CA, 2CA, 4CA, 5CA}. Decimal 3:2 CSAs form a redundant partial product, and a decimal 4:2 compressor accumulates that partial product with the previous sum-and-carry intermediate product. One multiplier digit is processed per iteration from LSD to MSD, and one nonredundant product digit emerges per iteration. # pp.904-906

### parallel_decimal_multiplication  (role: extends)
mechanism: The multiplier digits are recoded into {-5,...,+5}, producing p + 1 signed partial products from BCD-4221 multiples {1CA,...,5CA}. Four-bit binary 3:2 CSAs reduce the partial products, and decimal digit doublers correct binary carry digits. The sum-and-carry output is converted to BCD-8421 and resolved by a direct decimal CPA. # pp.910-914

## new_families
### decimal_fp_multiplication  (domain: decimal: decimal misc, closest: decimal_fma, why_not: decimal_fma requires an aligned addend and fused single rounding, while this mechanism performs multiplication only)
mechanism: Two decoded decimal64 operands undergo significand multiplication, parallel sign/exponent generation, leading-zero-based shift estimation, product alignment, sticky-bit generation, decimal rounding, exception handling, DPD encoding, and result assembly. The iterative implementation generates sticky information during accumulation and can extend its iterations for gradual underflow. The parallel implementation uses a post-CPA shifter and OR tree, and delegates subnormal calculation unless a bidirectional shifter is added. # pp.903-914

### decimal_compound_incrementer  (domain: decimal: decimal adders, closest: bcd_direct_addition, why_not: bcd_direct_addition produces one sum, while this mechanism produces T_P+0 and T_P+1 and supports increment insertion at the LSD or guard digit)
mechanism: A p-digit decimal compound adder accepts a decimal sum-and-carry representation and generates the truncated significand and its incremented value concurrently. Per-digit sum candidates are combined with digit generate/propagate signals and a carry network. Rounding selects either conditional sum, optionally left-shifts it by one digit, and concatenates g or (g + 1) mod 10. # pp.908-910

## space_gaps
* The `final_adder` slot of `iterative_decimal_multiplication` cannot name the paper's decimal compound adder because the slot admits only binary-adder families. # pp.908-910
* The `final_adder` slot of `parallel_decimal_multiplication` cannot name `bcd_direct_addition`, although the implementation uses a direct decimal CPA with a Kogge-Stone interdigit carry network. # pp.911-914
* The decimal vocabulary lacks a rounding slot for dual decimal significands T_P+0/T_P+1 and corrective one-digit left-shift selection. # pp.908-912
