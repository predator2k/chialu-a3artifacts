## summary
The paper proposes data-parallel FADDn and FDPn architectures that realign significands into fixed intrinsic-line regions before carry-save compression, internal addition, normalization, and one rounding operation. The realignment preserves bits during catastrophic cancellation and reduces multiple shifted-out fields to at most one sticky source. The synthesized designs reduce delay against networks of two-operand FP units while increasing area. (pp.346-354)

## families
### multi_term_fused_dot  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Each significand product enters as a carry-save pair `(ms, mc)`. Products are sorted by their summed exponents, shifted according to movable Realignment Lines, compressed into carry-save form, assimilated by one internal adder, normalized, and rounded once. Intrinsic Lines reserve separate regions within the internal adder, which prevents excessive shifting without extending the adder to the exponent range. No sticky bit exists before internal addition, so FDPn does not require the FADDn extreme-cancellation correction. (pp.346, 348-352)
choices:
  alignment_strategy: single_wide_window   # pp.348, 352
  rounding_contract: correctly_rounded   # pp.347, 353-354
  normalization_deferral: final_only   # p.352
new_choices:
  alignment_method: intrinsic_line_realignment — movable Realignment Lines select fixed Intrinsic-Line regions when exponent separation exceeds `wij`   # pp.348-351
  exponent_sorting: pairwise_weight_crossbar — pairwise exponent comparisons generate ranks that control a crossbar   # pp.350-351
slots:
  reduction: csa_tree   # p.352
parameters: `n>2`; implementations use `n=4,8`, Single Precision, and Double Precision; `len_dp = 2nf + Σceil(log2(n-i)) + log2(n)`; `2n` realignment shifters; one final rounding operation   # pp.347, 352-353

## new_families
### correctly_rounded_multioperand_addition  (domain: fp, closest: multi_term_fused_dot, why_not: multi_term_fused_dot requires product terms, while FADDn directly sums floating-point operands)
mechanism: FADDn sorts operands by exponent, assigns fixed Intrinsic Lines, and activates a new Realignment Line when exponent separation exceeds `wij`. Shifted significands are inverted/sign-extended as needed and compressed to carry-save form before one internal carry-propagated addition, normalization, and rounding. Cancellation detection selects the proper exponent and returns the smallest operand when all larger operands cancel and the sticky bit shows that the smallest operand was incompletely retained. (pp.348-351)
choices: operand_count: `n>2`, implemented `{4,8}`; alignment_method: `intrinsic_line_realignment`; exponent_sorting: `pairwise_weight_crossbar`; subnormal_handling: `flush_to_zero`; rounding_operations: `1`   # pp.347-351, 353

## space_gaps
* The vocabulary lacks a family for correctly rounded data-parallel floating-point multi-operand addition without multiplication. (pp.347, 350)
* `multi_term_fused_dot.alignment_strategy` lacks intrinsic-line realignment with threshold-controlled relocation. (pp.348-352)
* The multiplier-array structure used to produce each FDPn carry-save pair is not identified by enough detail to select a `multiplier` slot family. (pp.346, 352)

## open_questions
* The paper does not specify the internal carry-propagate-adder topology used by FADDn or FDPn.
* The paper does not state pipeline depth, initiation interval, clock frequency, power, or FPGA measurements.
* The random/corner-case distributions used for the fp32 verification are not specified.
