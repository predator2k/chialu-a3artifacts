## summary
The chapter proposes a parameterized, pipelined floating-point exponential based on multiplicative range reduction, tables, and piecewise polynomial approximation. The architecture provides faithful rounding with three guard bits and scales through quadruple precision on Xilinx/Altera FPGAs. Synthesis results compare frequency/latency/logic/DSP/memory costs with table-based, iterative, CORDIC, and commercial implementations.   # p.126, p.132, p.134

### lut_plus_poly  (role: proposes)
mechanism: X is reduced as X ≈ E·log 2+Y with Y in [−1/2,1/2), then Y is split into its k leading bits A and remaining bits Z. A table supplies eA. ## new_families
### fixed_real_constant_kcm  (domain: mul: integer multipliers, closest: behavioral_star, why_not: The mechanism is a LUT decomposition specialized to an integer multiplied by a real constant, which no multiplier family represents.)
mechanism: An n-bit integer is split into α-bit chunks. Separate LUTs store each shifted partial product 2^(iα)KEi, rounded directly from the real constant to a common guarded precision. The first table adds u/2, so truncating the accumulated partial products performs final rounding without a separate rounding adder.   # p.130, p.131
choices:
