## summary
The document implements one digit-recurrence datapath for floating-point division/square root and integer division/remainder by sharing operand normalization, recurrence, and result-normalization hardware. The unit also terminates special cases, short integer quotients, and exact results early. Shared units supporting at least double precision and producing at least 4 bits per cycle are smaller and faster than the evaluated separate units.

## families
### digit_recurrence_sqrt_combined  (role: extends)
mechanism: Integer operands are converted to magnitudes, leading-zero counted, and shifted into [1, 2) by the shifter used for subnormal floating-point inputs. A radix-2 or radix-4 recurrence keeps the residual as WC+WS, selects digits with a unified division/square-root selection circuit, updates the residual with a 3:2 carry-save adder, and forms U/UM with on-the-fly conversion. The postprocessor selects U or UM, corrects quotient/remainder signs, shifts integer results into the low bits, and normalizes/rounds floating-point results. # pp.743-746

### self_timed_variable_latency  (role: extends)
mechanism: Special cases terminate during preprocessing. Integer division uses leading-zero counts to compute the exact required step count and terminates immediately when the dividend is smaller than the divisor. Recurrence iterations terminate when WC+WS=0. Radix-2 additionally detects the exact residual states -2D for division and 2(-2S+K) for square root, preventing an infinite string of -1 digits and a false Inexact flag. Detection runs in parallel with recurrence logic. # pp.743-746

### restoring_nonrestoring  (role: compares)
mechanism: The separate integer baseline is radix-2 restoring division. Each step shifts one dividend bit into W, subtracts D with an N-bit carry-propagate adder, emits quotient bit 1 when the result is nonnegative, and otherwise restores W. Replicating the step hardware produces k bits per cycle. # pp.742,746

## space_gaps
* digit_recurrence_sqrt_combined lacks choices for integer division/remainder sharing, recurrence-copy count, and early-termination conditions. # pp.743-746
* restoring_nonrestoring.bits_per_cycle excludes the implemented 4-bit/cycle and 8-bit/cycle replicated baselines. # p.746

## open_questions
* The current implementation does not share the postnormalization shifter because that shifter is embedded in the FPU, although the paper identifies such sharing as a potential optimization. # p.747
* The paper identifies an N-bit carry-propagate residual adder in the restoring baseline but does not specify its adder family. # p.742
