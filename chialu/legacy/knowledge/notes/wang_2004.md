## summary
The paper proposes an IEEE-754R decimal floating-point divider that combines an operand-modification reciprocal seed, modified Newton-Raphson iterations, shared decimal multiplication, and specialized correct rounding. A synthesized decimal64 implementation has a 0.69 ns critical-path delay and a 150-cycle latency with a one-digit-per-cycle sequential multiplier. # p.11

## families
### decimal_newton  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The divider normalizes DPD operands after conversion to BCD, obtains a reciprocal seed from one DPD coefficient and a modified divisor, and applies truncated Newton-Raphson updates. Each update forms V by taking the nine’s complement of the retained fraction digits of X × Ri and then computes Ri+1 = Ri × V. A shared decimal multiplier performs seed generation, refinement, quotient generation, and the product used for rounding. # pp.3-10

### operand_modification_multiply  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: A table indexed by the k most significant divisor digits supplies C′ = 1/(XM + 5 × 10^−k−1)^2. Operand modification retains XM and takes the nine’s complement of the next k digits to form X′. Multiplication computes R0 = C′ × X′, which is truncated to 2k − 1 digits. DPD table inputs/outputs reduce coefficient storage. # pp.4-5

### iterative_decimal_multiplication  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The evaluated divider reuses Erle’s sequential decimal fixed-point multiplier. The multiplier accumulates partial products with carry-save addition, decimal (3:2) counters, and (4:2) compressors before a simplified decimal carry-propagate adder. A barrel shifter removes trailing zeros from Ri so multiplication may terminate early. # pp.10-11

## new_families
### product_digit_remainder_rounding  (domain: decimal misc, closest: back_multiply_remainder, why_not: the method determines the remainder sign/zero condition from selected digits of Q′′ × X rather than computing N = Y − Q′ × X)
mechanism: Q′ is truncated to n + 1 digits and incremented by 10^−(n+1) to form Q′′. The LSD of Y is compared with the corresponding digit of Q′′ × X to determine the remainder sign, while lower product digits determine whether the remainder is zero. An action table selects Q′′T, Q′′T + 10^−n, or Q′′T − 10^−n from the LSD/guard digit/remainder/rounding mode/result sign. # pp.7-9

## space_gaps
* decimal_newton lacks an iter_mult slot for the shared decimal multiplier used in seed generation/refinement/quotient generation/rounding.   # pp.5,10
* decimal_newton.final_round lacks product_digit_remainder_rounding, which avoids a full back-multiplied remainder subtraction.   # pp.7-9
* iterative_decimal_multiplication.final_adder cannot express the simplified decimal carry-propagate adder reported by the implementation.   # p.10
* operand_modification_multiply lacks a table_encoding choice for DPD versus BCD coefficient storage.   # p.5
* iterative_decimal_multiplication lacks an early-exit choice driven by short multiplier operands/trailing-zero removal.   # p.10

## open_questions
* The paper does not state the exact multiplicand-multiple set or multiplier-digit recoding used by the instantiated sequential multiplier.
* The paper does not identify the topology of the simplified decimal carry-propagate adder.
