## summary
The document presents a signed 54 × 54-bit full-array multiplier for IEEE double-precision significand processing, using modified Booth partial-product generation, a Wallace 4-2 compressor tree, and a CLA/carry-select final adder. The fabricated 0.5 μm CMOS chip reports a 10 ns multiplication time at 3.3 V and 870 mW at 100 MHz. (pp.125-126)
