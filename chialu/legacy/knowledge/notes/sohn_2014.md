## summary
The paper proposes a fused floating-point three-term adder that performs two additions with one rounding and supports five IEEE-754 rounding modes. The architecture combines overlapped exponent alignment, dual reduction, early normalization, three-input leading-zero anticipation, compound rounding, and an optional three-stage pipeline. # p.1, pp.3-7

## new_families
### fused_three_term_adder  (domain: dot, closest: multi_term_fused_dot, why_not: multi_term_fused_dot requires product terms, while this mechanism fuses three addends without multiplication)
mechanism: Three normalized floating-point operands are aligned by six parallel exponent-difference computations whose partial results drive the shifters. Dual 3:2 reduction trees form opposite-sign alternatives, and significand comparison selects a positive pair. Three-input leading-zero anticipation drives early normalization before a reduced-width prefix addition. Compound sum/sum+1 generation performs the single final rounding. The datapath can be unpipelined or divided into exponent/alignment, invert/normalize, and add/round stages. # pp.1, 3-7

### three_input_lza  (domain: fp, closest: lzd_cell_tree, why_not: lzd_cell_tree detects zeros in one value, while this mechanism anticipates cancellation directly from three redundant significand inputs)
mechanism: A bitwise pre-encoder represents the sum of three possibly inverted significands as a four-valued W vector, derives indicator symbols, and passes the resulting vector to an MSB-first leading-zero detector. The detector overlaps its lower levels with the normalization shifter. Pre-encoded bits also feed a comparison tree for dual-reduction selection and sign determination. Concurrent correction handles the possible 1-bit anticipation error. # p.5

## space_gaps
* The vocabulary lacks a fused floating-point multioperand-add family without product terms; this paper supplies a three-operand instance and states that the algorithms extend beyond three operands. # p.1
* The vocabulary lacks a direct three-input LZA component with concurrent 1-bit correction and reuse of pre-encoded bits for sign comparison. # p.5
* compound_flagged_prefix lacks a choice for overflow-dependent rounding positions, which this design requires for two, one, or no overflow. # p.6
* parallel_prefix lacks a choice for splitting early prefix levels across normalization to balance LZA delay. # p.4
