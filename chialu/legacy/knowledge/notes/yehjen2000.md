## summary
The paper combines a new radix-4 modified Booth encoding scheme, an arrival-time-optimized partial-product reduction tree, and a multiple-level conditional-sum final adder for parallel multiplication. The design reduces final-adder delay by up to 25 percent and total multiplier delay by around 8 percent under the reported normalized-delay evaluation. # p.692, p.699

## families
### booth_recoded_parallel  (role: extends)
mechanism: A three-bit radix-4 encoder produces X1_b, X2_b, and Z controls for an n-bit decoder. The decoder generates partial products in two unit delays using 18 transistors. Row_LSB and Neg_cin are combined and minimized so the extra partial-product term moves from bit n-2 to bit n-1 and the LSB portion becomes more regular. A TDM-generated partial-product reduction tree uses the resulting unequal arrival profile. # p.693-p.696

### conditional_sum  (role: extends)
mechanism: The MLCSMA algorithm consumes the two-row PPRT outputs and their arrival times from LSB to MSB. It recursively creates two-bit conditional-carry sections when timing slack spans another level, uses full adders where ripple propagation is fast enough, and uses a conditional-sum structure for the last block. Intermediate r/g terms permit block boundaries at arbitrary bit positions rather than powers of two. The algorithm optimizes the adder bit by bit in linear time. # p.696-p.699

  selection_radix: 2   # p.696-p.698

  carry_sum_hybridization: CCA_except_final_CSMA — saves area while retaining CSMA speed at the last block   # p.696-p.698

## space_gaps
* booth_recoded_parallel lacks a final-carry-propagate-adder slot for the MLCSMA, although final addition is one of the paper's three multiplier stages and a central optimization target. # p.692, p.696-p.700
* conditional_sum lacks choices for unequal input-arrival profiles, algorithmic block-boundary synthesis, and conditional-carry/conditional-sum hybridization. # p.696-p.699
* csa_reduction_tree lacks choices for TDM arrival-time-driven column-compressor synthesis and heterogeneous full-adder cell selection. # p.692, p.695, p.699
