## summary
The paper presents a parametric error analysis for Goldschmidt division with imprecise intermediate computations and strict directed rounding. The analysis derives closed error bounds and applies them to an alternative AMD-K7 FP-DIV microarchitecture with smaller multiplier operands and a less accurate reciprocal seed. The proposed parameters retain correct IEEE rounding through back multiplication while reducing estimated hardware cost.

The two products within an iteration are independent, so they can execute in parallel or pipeline, while errors from the imprecise products accumulate because the iteration is not self-correcting. ## space_gaps
* The `goldschmidt` family lacks an intermediate-rounding choice covering round-to-nearest/directed/strict-directed policies. # p.4, p.6
* The `goldschmidt` family lacks a per-operation precision schedule for unequal multiplicand widths and separately bounded intermediate errors. # p.3-7
* The `goldschmidt.iterations` domain excludes the one-iteration single-precision configuration. # p.6
* The `goldschmidt` family lacks an initial reciprocal-error budget, although the seed accuracy is optimized jointly with multiplier dimensions. # p.2, p.7

## open_questions
* The exact seed-error bounds, precision pairs, optimal error parameters, and some percentage glyphs are corrupted in the supplied document text and must be verified against a legible PDF.
* The paper identifies a lookup table for the reciprocal seed but does not specify enough table structure to select `monolithic_rom`, `bipartite_rom`, or another seed family.
