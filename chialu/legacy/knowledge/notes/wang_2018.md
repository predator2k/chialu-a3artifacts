## summary
The document demonstrates DNN training with FP8 operands/multiplications, FP16 chunk-based dot-product accumulation, and FP16 stochastic-rounded weight updates while maintaining test accuracy close to FP32 baselines across six networks (pp.3-6). A 14 nm implementation reports less than 5% chunking energy overhead and 2-4 times higher efficiency than pure FP16 computation (p.9).

## families
### fp8_training_datapath  (role: proposes)
mechanism: GEMM dot products multiply two FP8 vectors and accumulate the products in FP16. Each long dot product is divided into chunks; intra-chunk sums are accumulated first, and the partial sums are then accumulated into the final result. FP16 AXPY weight-update operations use floating-point stochastic rounding, whose rounding-error magnitude scales with the exponent. The hierarchy reduces the theoretical accumulation-error bound from O(N) to O(N/CL + CL) and limits swamping (p.4).

errors_and_checks: No maximum-ulp or arithmetic-error contract is reported. Table 1 reports empirical test errors close to FP32 across six models; Figure 3 reports that nearest-rounded FP16 accumulation stops at length ≥ 4096 for ChunkSize=1, while ChunkSize >= 32 compensates the swamping error (p.5).
conditions: Chunking is required for ResNet50 convergence, and Gradient GEMM is the most accumulation-sensitive operation when chunking is absent (p.8). Chunk sizes from 64 to 256 give the smallest normalized L2-distance in the tested CIFAR10-ResNet Gradient GEMMs; size 64 is used for implementation convenience (p.8). ResNet18/ResNet50 ImageNet inputs require FP16 representation or incur approximately 2% accuracy degradation (p.7). The final layer uses FP16 for robustness and accounts for < 1% of DNN FLOPS (p.7). The experiments use a loss-scaling factor of 1000, and the final layer remains FP16 (p.6).
evidence: §2.2-2.3 and Figures 2-3 (pp.3-5); Table 1 and Figure 4 (pp.5-6); Tables 2-4 and Figures 5-6 (pp.7-8); §4.4 and Figure 7 (p.9)

## space_gaps
* fp8_training_datapath needs an accumulator-format/layout choice because this work uses custom FP16 (1,6,9), rather than specifying only the broad `fp16` precision (p.3).
* fp8_training_datapath needs a chunk-size choice because accumulation accuracy and energy depend on chunk length, with 64 used experimentally and 64-256 favored by the reported error study (pp.5,8).
* fp8_training_datapath lacks a first/last-layer precision-policy choice for retaining sensitive inputs/outputs or whole GEMMs at FP16 (pp.6-7).

## open_questions
* The document does not state whether each FP8 multiplication product is exact before FP16 conversion or rounded in FP8, so the family description’s exact-product property remains unsettled (pp.4-5).
* The document does not identify the multiplier, accumulator-adder, or stochastic-rounder microarchitecture used in the 14 nm core (p.9).
* The hardware study reports relative efficiency and energy overhead but no absolute area/power/throughput values or device dimensions (p.9).
