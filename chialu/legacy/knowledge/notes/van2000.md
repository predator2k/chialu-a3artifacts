## summary
The paper proposes a data-dependent error-compensation method for an n-bit two’s-complement fixed-width multiplier that returns the n most significant product bits. The selected generalized index and Type 2 binary thresholding reduce maximum/average error and error variance relative to the K-G-A and J-K fixed-width multipliers while retaining an area close to half that of a standard multiplier. A 35-tap speech FIR filter demonstrates the multiplier with 8-bit operands and a 32-bit accumulator.

## families
### truncated_fixed_width  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The multiplier partitions the 2n-bit Baugh–Wooley product into retained most-significant and discarded least-significant sections. A realizable bias approximates the ideally rounded discarded section through a coarse-adjustment term and a statistically selected fine-adjustment term. The proposed structure uses Type 2 binary thresholding with the generalized index Q=2^(n-1)+1. The compensation adds one when the selected index is less than n and omits that increment when the index equals n. # pp.1113-1116
