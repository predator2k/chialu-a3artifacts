## summary
The paper replaces conventional bit-pair carry generation with a complementing-signal look-ahead recurrence that examines neighboring bit pairs. The demonstrated 32-bit adder requires three logic levels under the stated fan-in/fan-out constraints, while an address-generation application requires four rather than six logic levels. # pp.156-160

## families
### ling_prefix  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The scheme forms a new carry H_i from a local complementing signal k_i and a remotely propagated H_{i+1} controlled by transmitting-enable switch T_{i+1}. H_i serves as both a carry and a complementing signal. Four-bit groups form look-ahead signals, and the sum is generated in parallel by selecting a conditional value after H becomes available. The implementation uses the Sklansky conditional-sum method for the final sum expression. # pp.156-160
