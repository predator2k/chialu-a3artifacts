## summary
The document establishes packed 16-bit SIMD arithmetic in existing 32-bit and 64-bit PA-RISC integer datapaths by blocking carries at lane boundaries and partitioning the ALU preshifter. The document also establishes per-lane signed/unsigned saturation, shift-and-add, and round-to-odd average instructions. The PA-7100LC implementation adds less than 0.2 percent silicon area and accelerates several multimedia applications by 1.9 to 2.7 times.

### partitioned_carry_chain  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The PA-7100LC partitions each 32-bit ALU into two 16-bit lanes by blocking carry propagation from the low halfword into the high halfword during packed addition/subtraction. A 64-bit PA-RISC 2.0 ALU similarly executes four independent 16-bit operations. Existing registers, instruction width, functional units, and memory paths carry packed operands without pipeline/register/memory changes. # p.25-26

### saturating_clamp  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Each packed add/subtract/shift-and-add lane can clip positive overflow to the maximum representable value and negative overflow to the minimum representable value. Signed saturation uses signed 16-bit operands/results. Unsigned saturation also permits one unsigned operand, one signed operand, and an unsigned result, which clips negative values to zero. Sequences of one to three saturating instructions clip results to arbitrary bounds. # p.27-29

## new_families
### partitioned_shift_add  (domain: shift: sub-word SIMD, closest: partitioned_carry_chain, why_not: Lane-local preshifting fused with addition is a distinct mechanism from blocking interlane carry.)
mechanism: The ALU preshifter is partitioned alongside the adder so each 16-bit lane shifts one operand left or right by 1, 2, or 3 bits and then adds the other operand. Left shift-and-add implements multiplication by small integer constants. Right shift-and-add implements multiplication by fractional constants. Signed saturation is available, and the partitioning does not affect PA-7100LC cycle time. # p.26-27

### rounded_subword_average  (domain: shift: sub-word SIMD, closest: partitioned_carry_chain, why_not: Fused addition, carry insertion, shifting, and round-to-odd define behavior beyond carry-boundary partitioning.)
mechanism: Each lane adds two 16-bit values and shifts the sum right by one bit. The carry-out enters as the result's most significant bit, so the average cannot overflow. An OR of the two least significant sum bits before shifting implements round-to-odd without additional delay. # p.27
