## summary
The paper formulates multidimensional dynamic programs that find minimum-latency carry-skip and variable-block-size block carry-lookahead adders. The formulations retain nondominated delay vectors and support delay models that include gate fanin/fanout rather than counting logic levels alone.

## families
### carry_skip  (role: extends)
mechanism: Full adders are grouped into blocks with group-propagate-controlled carry-skip mechanisms, and blocks are grouped recursively into higher-level stages. The dynamic program retains nondominated delay tuples representing internal/generate/assimilate/skip paths and constructs configurations by appending blocks or stages. Pruning by domination/fathoming limits the retained configurations. The setup-aware formulation charges group-propagate setup time to the generate path, which makes that component exact while potentially underestimating three other components. # pp.921-925

### carry_lookahead  (role: extends)
mechanism: Variable-size block carry-lookahead adders are built recursively by filling an m-input carry-lookahead generator from its least-significant position. The dynamic program retains five delay/load components for complete adders and eight components for partial blocks, including separate carry-input and overall worst-case delays. These components account for additional carry-input loading when a subadder becomes part of a larger block. A three-dimensional construction volume and upper-bound/dominance pruning produce minimum-latency configurations. # pp.925-929

## space_gaps
* carry_skip lacks a choice for the delay-model class, including size-dependent skip delay and fanin/fanout contributions. # pp.920,922-925
* carry_skip lacks a choice for how group-propagate setup time is incorporated or bounded. # p.924
* carry_lookahead levels cannot express a recursively optimized, technology-bounded number of levels because its domain ends at 4. # pp.925-929
* carry_lookahead lacks a choice for a linear fanin/fanout gate-delay model. # p.926

## open_questions
* The paper does not state a fixed maximum number of carry-skip levels, so the merge pass must not assign a numeric skip_levels value.
* The setup-aware carry-skip formulation may underestimate three delay components, so those values must not be treated as exact.
* The paper reports converted 8-bit/16-bit delays for a “typical 1.5 μm CMOS technology,” but it does not identify a fabricated implementation.
