## summary
The document reports separate unrounded-multiply/add pipelines that retain 4-cycle binary64 FMA latency while reducing add/multiply and dependent sum-of-products latency (p.97). The document also reports a single-cycle binary64 adder, an ordered SVE reduction, bfloat16 dot/matrix operations, and a GPU organization that saves about 33% dynamic power (pp.97-98).

### bridge_fma  (role: proposes)

### bf16_fma_datapath  (role: instantiates)
