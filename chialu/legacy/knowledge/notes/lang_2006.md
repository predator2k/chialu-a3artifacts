## summary
The paper proposes a combinational 16-digit radix-10 multiplier whose recoded partial products require only 2x and 5x precomputations. Radix-10 carry-save adders/counters reduce the partial products, and a parallel-prefix carry-save-to-BCD converter produces an exact 32-digit product. The synthesized 90 nm implementation has a 2.65 ns critical path and occupies 0.30 mm2.

## families
### parallel_decimal_multiplication  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Each multiplier digit is recoded as yi = yHi + yLi, where yH ∈ {0, 5, 10} and yL ∈ {−2, −1, 0, 1, 2}. The resulting partial-product generator selects precomputed x/2x/5x/10x values and radix-10 complements for negative terms. Six levels of radix-10 carry-save adders and carry counters reduce 16 carry-save partial products. A parallel-prefix carry-save-to-BCD converter produces the 32-digit product.

### decimal_multioperand_addition  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Radix-10 carry-save adders reduce two BCD digit vectors plus a carry-bit vector into one BCD digit vector and one carry-bit vector. The first tree level reduces eight pairs of partial-product sum vectors while leaving eight carry vectors. Radix-10 carry counters combine those carry vectors into decimal digits. Further radix-10 CSA levels reduce all outputs to a final carry-save pair.

### bcd_direct_addition  (role: extends)
mechanism: The final converter accepts a 2n-digit BCD vector A and a 2n-bit carry vector D. Its first stage computes decimal propagate/generate signals and both digit candidates s0i = ⟨ai + di⟩10 and s1i = ⟨ai + di + 1⟩10. A parallel-prefix stage computes carries, and a final stage selects s0i or s1i. Prepending a simplified radix-10 CSA generalizes the converter into a two-operand BCD carry-propagate adder.

## space_gaps
* `parallel_decimal_multiplication.final_adder` and `decimal_multioperand_addition.root_adder` exclude `bcd_direct_addition`, although this implementation terminates both structures with a radix-10 carry-save-to-BCD parallel-prefix adder.   # p.315
* `decimal_multioperand_addition` lacks a choice for the 8-input carry-counter array used alongside the radix-10 CSA tree.   # pp.314-316
* `parallel_decimal_multiplication.multiplier_recoding` lacks the paper’s explicit yH ∈ {0,5,10}, yL ∈ {−2,−1,0,1,2} recoding value.   # p.313

## open_questions
* Table III groups the reported 155,000 μm2 partial-product-generation area and 135,000 μm2 tree area across several rows, so per-block areas must not be inferred.   # p.317
* The paper does not report power, pipeline-register area, or pipeline-register power.
