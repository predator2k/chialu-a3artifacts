## summary
The paper proposes three transistor-level approximate full-adder cells based on XOR/XNOR gates and pass-transistor multiplexers for low-power inexact computing (pp.690-691). TSMC 65nm simulations compare AXA1/AXA2/AXA3 with a 10-transistor accurate full adder using transistor count, static/dynamic power, Sum/Cout delay, PDP and total error distance (pp.691-693).

## families
### lower_part_approximate  (role: extends)
mechanism: The paper establishes `xor_xnor_axa` approximate cells rather than a complete exact-upper/approximate-lower word architecture. AXA1 implements XOR with an inverter and two pass transistors and approximates both Sum and Cout. AXA2 combines a four-transistor XNOR gate with a pass-transistor block and produces exact Cout. AXA3 extends AXA2 with two pass transistors to improve Sum accuracy while retaining exact Cout. Each design removes transistors from a 10-transistor accurate full adder to reduce node capacitance and logic complexity (pp.690-691).

errors_and_checks: Error distance is the absolute arithmetic distance between an approximate output and the exact output; total error distance sums ED over all eight full-adder input combinations (p.690). AXA1 has accurate Sum and Cout for 4 of 8 combinations; AXA2 has accurate Sum for 4 of 8 and accurate Cout for all combinations; AXA3 has accurate Sum for 6 of 8 and accurate Cout for all combinations (pp.691-692). No fault model, detection coverage, false-alarm behavior or checker is reported.
conditions: AXA1 provides the shortest Cout delay and lowest plotted PDP, but its static power exceeds the accurate-adder baseline (pp.692-693). AXA2 has the lowest transistor count, but its Cout delay is slightly longer than the accurate-adder delay (p.693). AXA3 has the lowest dynamic power and total error distance among the AXAs, with intermediate Cout delay (p.693). Pass-transistor threshold loss produces non-full-swing outputs and lower noise margin, so additional output drivers may be required (pp.691-693). The designs apply to applications that tolerate lower numerical accuracy (pp.690, 693).
evidence: §II; §III.1-III.4; Table 1; §IV; Figs. 5-8; Tables 2-3; §V.

## space_gaps
* The vocabulary lacks a standalone approximate full-adder-cell family or component slot; this paper specifies AXA cells without fixing the `lower_width`, `carry_to_upper` or exact `upper_adder` required by `lower_part_approximate` (pp.691-693).
* `cell_variant` should distinguish AXA1/AXA2/AXA3 because the variants have different Sum/Cout functions, transistor counts, delays, power and total error distances (pp.691-693).

## open_questions
* The paper does not specify which bits of a multi-bit adder use AXA cells or whether an exact upper section is retained.
* Fig. 7 reports PDP only graphically, so exact PDP values are unavailable from the printed text.
