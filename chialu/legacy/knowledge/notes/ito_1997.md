## summary
The paper replaces the multiply-add of piecewise-linear reciprocal/reciprocal-square-root/square-root approximation with one multiplication against a bit-modified operand (pp.495-497). The method retains the conventional linear approximation accuracy while reducing coefficient ROM and potentially removing one clock cycle (p.498).

## families
### operand_modification_multiply  (role: proposes)
mechanism: The upper m operand bits select one coefficient B1, D1, or F1 from a 2^m-entry ROM. A modifier complements lower operand bits for reciprocal and complements plus shifts bits for reciprocal square root and square root. Multiplication of the coefficient by the modified operand replaces the conventional coefficient multiplication and addition. The iteration multiplier can be reused, so the added hardware is the ROM and modifier; a dedicated reciprocal multiplier may instead be (t + 1)-bits by t-bits (pp.496-497).

errors_and_checks: The reciprocal bound is eP < (1/p^3)·2^(-2m-3) + Y·2^(-t-1) (p.496, (7)). Equations (14) and (19) bound reciprocal-square-root and square-root approximation errors, respectively (p.497). The paper reports the same approximation accuracy as conventional linear approximation but gives no end-to-end ulp guarantee or fault check (pp.496-498).
conditions: The method applies to initial mantissa approximations for multiplicative floating-point division and square root using Newton-Raphson or Goldschmidt refinement (pp.495-497). The method removes the addition while retaining linear-approximation accuracy, so one clock cycle may be saved (p.498). The ROM is about half the original-linear size and about two thirds the improved-linear size (p.498). Reusing the iteration multiplier requires no dedicated arithmetic hardware beyond the ROM and operand modifier (pp.496-497). An extra coefficient table and addition can increase accuracy or reduce m, but that modified method restores an addition (pp.496-498).
evidence: Sections 2-4; Fig. 1; equations (4)-(7), (11)-(14), and (17)-(19); Tables 1-2 (pp.496-498).

## space_gaps
* `operand_modification_multiply.function` lacks `sqrt`, although the paper applies the mechanism directly to square-root approximation as well as reciprocal and reciprocal-square-root seeds (pp.497-498).
* `operand_modification_multiply` lacks choices for the operand transformation and for reuse versus dedication of the multiplier, which determine modifier hardware and multiplier sizing (pp.496-497).

## open_questions
* The technology/device, area, power, physical delay, and implemented clock period are not reported.
* The claimed one-cycle saving is architectural; the paper does not report a fabricated or synthesized pipeline measurement (p.498).
