## summary
The chapter defines frequency-driven FPGA ripple-carry pipelining and proposes AAM/CAI/CCA short-latency architectures derived from carry-select and carry-increment addition. Dedicated FPGA carry chains make ripple carry preferable at ordinary widths, while the proposed architectures reduce latency and sometimes resources for sufficiently wide additions. Resource formulas predict synthesized costs within about 1-3%.   # p.61, p.63, p.77-p.79

### compare_compare_add  (domain: adder, closest: carry_increment, why_not: CCA computes both speculative carries with comparators and performs one complete final addition rather than incrementing a stored speculative sum.)
mechanism: Each block compares Xk with not(Yk) using ≥ and > to produce its speculative one-carry and zero-carry outputs. A CCC/CR network determines the actual block carry, which feeds a final full addition Xk + Yk + ck. Five-input LUTs merge recovery into the final addition and permit half-width comparator carry chains.
