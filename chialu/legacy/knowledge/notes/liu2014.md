## summary
The document proposes an approximate binary multiplier whose partial products are accumulated by error-signaling approximate adders with nearest-neighbor carry propagation. Configurable recovery OR-accumulates selected MSBs of the error vectors and adds the accumulated error to the product with an accurate adder. An exhaustive 8 × 8 evaluation and 16 × 16 FPGA/28nm CMOS implementations report accuracy, delay, and power against approximate and exact multipliers.

## families
### approximate_compressor  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Input preprocessing replaces each operand-bit pair with Ȧi = Ai + Bi and Ḃi = AiBi. Each approximate adder cuts a carry chain after one neighboring position, produces sum Si, and emits error bit Ei. An adder tree accumulates partial products in parallel. OR gates approximately accumulate the error vectors, and a conventional accurate adder adds selected MSBs of the accumulated error to the tree output. The selected MSB count configures the accuracy/complexity tradeoff. # p.1–2

errors_and_checks: ED = |M′ − M|, NMED = MED/Mmax, RED = ED/M, MRED is the mean RED, and ER is the percentage of erroneous outputs. Exhaustive simulation covers all inputs of the 8 × 8 multiplier. The approximate adder satisfies S′ = S + E, E is non-negative, and the approximate sum never exceeds the accurate sum. OR accumulation can lose error magnitude when multiple error vectors contain a 1 at the same position. No maximum-error bound is reported.   # p.2–3
conditions: NMED/MRED decrease sharply when the recovery count rises from 0 to 6 and then decrease more slowly. The authors identify 6 or 7 recovery MSBs as an accuracy/complexity tradeoff. ER remains high even when average error magnitude is small. Error recovery requires a final accurate adder, and selecting more error-vector MSBs increases recovery complexity. Shared logic between neighboring ASIC cells reduces area, and the paper reports no additional area overhead for the shorter analytical tree delay without giving an area measurement.   # p.3–4

## space_gaps
* `approximate_compressor` lacks a value that distinguishes an error-signaling two-input approximate-adder tree from an inexact compressor tree.   # p.2
* `approximate_compressor` lacks choices for OR versus exact error accumulation and for the configurable number of recovered MSBs.   # p.2–3
* `approximate_compressor` lacks a final accurate-adder slot for adding the accumulated error vector to the approximate product.   # p.2
