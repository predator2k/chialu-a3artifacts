## summary
The article reports the 4.4-GHz IBM z10 processor and its fixed-point, binary floating-point, and decimal floating-point execution units. The arithmetic evidence establishes a fully hardware decimal floating-point unit and residue checking for arithmetic elements, but it does not disclose their internal datapaths.

### commercial_decimal_fpu  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The DFU is a distinct execution unit that implements IEEE-754R decimal floating-point and S/360 legacy fixed-point decimal operations entirely in hardware. The z9 predecessor implemented decimal floating-point instructions with hardware and millicode. ### residue  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The processor uses residue checks for arithmetic elements as part of a fine-grained suite that also includes ECC, parity, functional checking, state checking, and local replication. The recovery unit collects fault-detection signals and oversees recovery actions. The residue modulus, generator, protected operations, and comparison structure are not disclosed. # p.22, p.27
