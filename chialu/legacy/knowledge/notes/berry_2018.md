## summary
The document describes the IBM z14 CP/SC chips, cache/system topology, reliability, physical-design changes, clock gating, and cryptographic assists rather than arithmetic-unit microarchitectures (pp.36-38). The CP operates at 5.2GHz in Global Foundries 14nm 14HP SOI FinFET technology, while the redesigned AES pipeline computes two rounds in three cycles (p.36).
