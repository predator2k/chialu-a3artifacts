## summary
The document presents POSGEN, a tool that generates and pipelines configurable POSIT addition/subtraction/multiplication operators for Intel FPGAs (pp.138, 142). The generated operators reach frequencies comparable to two IEEE-754 generators but require more ALMs and pipeline stages (pp.142-144).

## families
### posit_adder_multiplier  (role: proposes)
mechanism: POSGEN decodes each variable-length POSIT into sign/regime/exponent/mantissa fields before arithmetic and encodes the result afterward. Multiplication computes the mantissa product, normalizes it by at most one right shift, and adds the operand exponents/regimes. Addition/subtraction orders the operands, aligns the smaller mantissa, adds or subtracts, locates the leading set bit, normalizes, and adjusts the exponent/regime. The generator pipelines the resulting VHDL operator. (pp.140-142)

### lzd_cell_tree  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The POSIT decoder determines the regime by counting the consecutive equal bits after the sign. One CLZ module handles both bit polarities by inverting the operand when its leading bit is set. The implementation divides the operand into 4-bit chunks and combines counts through an adder tree whose accumulator gains one bit at each level. The decoded count controls a right shift that reveals the exponent and mantissa. (p.140)
