## summary
The paper proposes an iterative fixed-point BCD multiplier that recodes operand digits into signed-magnitude digits from −5 to +5 and generates restricted-range partial products on demand. A Svoboda signed-digit adder accumulates one word-by-digit partial product per iteration, and a final conversion produces the BCD product. (§§3–6)

## families
### iterative_decimal_multiplication  (role: proposes)
mechanism: Each BCD operand digit is recoded into signed-magnitude form from −5 to +5. The multiplicand is recoded in parallel, while multiplier digits are recoded as they are consumed from LSD to MSD. An n+1-block word-by-digit multiplier generates an overlapped signed-magnitude partial product, which is recoded for a Svoboda signed-digit adder and accumulated iteratively. Completed low-order digits emerge during accumulation, and the remaining signed-digit intermediate product is converted to BCD in two final cycles. (§§3–6)

### generalized_signed_digit  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: Each iteration adds an n+1-digit partial product to an n+1-digit intermediate product with parallel Svoboda digit adders. Each five-bit digit represents a value from −6 through +6, and the additive inverse is obtained by one’s complement. Each addition produces a sum digit and a transfer digit for the next position. The completed LSD is converted to BCD while accumulation continues, and the remaining signed-digit word is converted after the last iteration. (§5, Tables 3–4)

  additive_inverse: ones_complement — complementing all five encoded bits produces the additive inverse   # §5, Table 3
slots: none

## space_gaps
* `iterative_decimal_multiplication.multiple_set` lacks a value for generating restricted-range digit products on demand without stored multiplicand multiples. (§2, §4)
* `iterative_decimal_multiplication.accumulator` lacks `generalized_signed_digit`, which fills the accumulator role in the presented design. (§5)
* `generalized_signed_digit.radix` lacks radix 10. (§5)
* `generalized_signed_digit.digit_encoding` lacks the five-bit Svoboda decimal code whose additive inverse is obtained by one’s complement. (§5, Table 3)
* `iterative_decimal_multiplication` lacks choices for operand recoding and overlapped partial-product encoding, which are central tunables of this design. (§§3–4)
