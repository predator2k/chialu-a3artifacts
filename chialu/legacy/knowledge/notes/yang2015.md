## summary
The paper proposes three approximate 4-2 compressors, ACCI1/ACCI2/ACCI3, constrained for low error rate. The compressors are combined with truncation in unsigned 8x8 Dadda multipliers, which reduce power/area/delay while maintaining high SNR/SSIM in image sharpening. (p.183, pp.184-186)

## families
### approximate_compressor_tree  (role: proposes)
mechanism: ACCI1/ACCI2/ACCI3 approximate the truth table of an accurate compressor after ignoring its carry-in/carry-out signals. ACCI1 changes the all-one output from `100` to `11`; ACCI2 also changes the `0011` case to simplify the sum logic; ACCI3 removes another sum term. Eight ACCIs reduce the next four columns above four truncated columns, while accurate compressors reduce the more significant partial products. (pp.183-184)

### truncated_fixed_width  (role: instantiates)
mechanism: The unsigned 8x8 Dadda multiplier discards its four least-significant partial-product columns. The next four columns use approximate compressors, while accurate compressors handle the remaining more-significant columns. The paper combines truncation with approximation because the proposed compressors have lower error rates than the prior approximate compressor. (p.184)

## open_questions

* The paper identifies M1-M3 as multipliers using ACCI1-ACCI3, but the supplied text does not explicitly state the ordering beyond the `M1~M3` notation. (p.185)
* The carry-propagate adder structure is not specified. (p.184)
