## summary
The document proposes a segmented approximate adder whose parallel carry-skip prediction lowers error rate and critical-path delay, with a forced-output stage that reduces error magnitude (pp.130-133). A 90 nm implementation and a neuromorphic character-recognition evaluation report a 0.18% random-input error rate for the 16-bit configuration and up to 48.5% neuron-level energy savings over RCA (pp.134-137).

## families
### segmented_carry_speculative  (role: proposes)
mechanism: An n-bit adder is divided into m=⌈n/k⌉ k-bit blocks containing concurrent sub-adders and sub-carry generators. Each sub-adder predicts its carry-in from the two preceding block carry-outs. A multiplexer skips across the nearer block when all of that block's propagate signals are true. Prediction fails only for carry-propagation chains longer than 2k. The error-magnitude stage forces the sums of two consecutive all-propagate blocks to one, reducing the significance of a missed carry without another cycle. An optional propagate/generate-based prefix correction reconstructs an exact result in one additional cycle. (pp.131-135)

errors_and_checks: Under independent random input bits, a prediction error requires a carry-propagation chain longer than 2k; the paper derives Perr(n,k) by inclusion-exclusion and reports 0.18% for n=16, k=4 (pp.131-132). The reduction stage changes the illustrated error significance from 2/17 to 2/123 and reduces the stated worst-case error magnitude from 2^(n-k) to 2^(n-3k) (p.133). Optional propagate/generate detection plus prefix correction produces error-free results in one additional cycle; false-alarm behavior is UNKNOWN (pp.130, 135).
conditions: Larger k increases delay while reducing power/error rate for fixed n; increasing n at fixed k leaves delay nearly constant while error rate rises slowly (p.134). The approximate mode targets error-resilient DSP/neuromorphic computation rather than applications requiring exact addition (p.130). The neuromorphic result depends on 3-bit weights, 5-bit model parameters, 16-bit membrane potentials, and the evaluated unsupervised character-recognition network (pp.133-136). ETAII consumes less energy in the comparison but its higher error rate prevents successful training, while the proposed adder trains all letters successfully (pp.135-136).
evidence: §2, Eqs. (1)-(12), Figs. 1-4, pp.131-133; §3, Figs. 5-6, pp.133-134; §4, Tables 1-3 and Figs. 7-10, pp.134-137.

## space_gaps
* segmented_carry_speculative lacks a choice for the number of preceding carry generators included in parallel carry-skip prediction; the paper fixes two and permits more (pp.131-132).
* segmented_carry_speculative lacks an error-correction slot for the optional parallel_prefix circuit used to reconstruct exact results in an additional cycle (p.135).

## open_questions
* The topology of the prefix adder used for exact recovery is unspecified (p.135).
* The paper does not state whether the optional exact-recovery circuit is runtime-configurable or selected at implementation time (pp.130, 135).
