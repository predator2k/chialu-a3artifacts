## summary
The paper proposes a fixed-width modified Booth multiplier that modifies the partial-product matrix and estimates the discarded portion's carry with a simplified sorting network. The compensation makes the error distribution more symmetric around zero and reduces mean/mean-square error for lossy DSP and multimedia applications.

## families
### truncated_fixed_width  (role: proposes)
mechanism: An n×n signed multiplier returns the n most significant product bits. The design pre-adds selected least-significant partial-product/sign-extension terms, removes the remaining truncated partial products, and estimates their carry into the retained matrix from modified Booth encoder outputs. An SC-generator derived from an odd-even merge sorting network produces the compensation bits, which enter the Dadda reduction tree with the retained partial products. (pp.53-58)

### booth_recoded_parallel  (role: extends)
mechanism: Modified Booth encoding groups multiplier bits into triplets and selects 0, X, 2X, or their negations for each partial-product row. A negative row is formed by bit inversion plus a correction bit added at the least-significant position of the next row. The proposed fixed-width design transforms the sign-extension/boundary terms before truncation and reduces the retained matrix with a Dadda tree. (pp.53-58)
