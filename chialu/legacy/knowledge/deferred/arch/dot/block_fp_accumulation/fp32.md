# fp32

Hybrid block floating point: each block or tile dot product runs in
fixed point over mantissas aligned to one shared exponent, its
fixed-point sum is converted to FP32 by adding the two shared
exponents once and normalizing, and FP32 ALUs cascade or accumulate
the per-block results, so a long dot product sums the results of
several bounding-box-sized units and every value returns to floating
point after each block.

FP32 inter-block accumulation keeps the fixed-point inner loop short
and lets the block stay small, which is where quantization error is
lowest, at the cost of one floating-point operation per 2N
fixed-point operations for an N by N tile and a floating-point
terminal accumulator per dot product; on the Stratix 10 NX the three
terminal accumulators fit in one block in the BFP16 and BFP24 modes.
Training with 8- or 12-bit mantissas and 24 by 24 tiles stays within
0.5 percent of FP32 across the tested image and language models, and
the 8-bit FPGA prototype reaches 8.5 times the throughput of an FP16
multiply-add accelerator on the same device. It is the pick for DNN
training and inference kernels where tiles are converted just before
each matrix product; a filter or transform with a constant
coefficient vector keeps the single wide fixed-point accumulator.

The library realizes this choice as a pin of the generated block_fp_accumulation module (`chialu/targets/rtl/families/dot.py`); the family card names the construction.
