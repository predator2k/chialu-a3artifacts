# e8m0

One 8-bit power-of-two scale with no mantissa (E8M0) is shared by a
block of 32 elements, each an FP8 (E4M3 or E5M2), FP6, FP4 or INT8
value, so a block is X times k scalar elements. Dot-product inputs are
converted to MX along the reduction dimension, while dot-product
outputs and vector operations remain scalar bf16 or fp32; training
quantizes the forward and backward dot products and keeps fp32 master
weights.

It is the pick as the interchange format the OCP specification fixes:
MXINT8 direct-cast inference matches FP32 within the reported
deviation on GPT3-175B and LLaMA-7B tasks, MXFP6_E3M2 trains GPT
models from 20M to 1.5B parameters with FP32-tuned hyperparameters
unchanged (2.75 against 2.74 loss at 1.5B), and MXFP4 weights with
MXFP6 activations and gradients add one hundredth of loss more. The
conversion rounds half to nearest even for inference and half away
from zero for training, and recipes beyond the reference algorithm
are implementation-defined. The two-level sibling spends a
microexponent per element pair for more fidelity per stored bit at
the cost of shift logic in the tree.

The library realizes this choice as a pin of the generated mx_microscaling_dot module (`chialu/targets/rtl/families/dot.py`); the family card names the construction.
