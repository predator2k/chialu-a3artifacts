# fp32

The Arria 10 single-precision block: an fp32 multiplier overlaid on the
27x27 fixed-point multiplier pipeline formed from two 18x18
multipliers, reusing its compressor and carry-propagate structures,
plus a separate single-precision adder shaped to the block's aspect
ratio, with dedicated chain connections that compose blocks into
reduction trees. Subnormals flush to zero, no flags are produced and
FMA is not supported.

This is the baseline mode, present on Arria 10, Stratix 10 and Agilex,
and the one to pin when the workload is single-precision multiply-add
or reduction and the fixed-point modes must stay intact. Its cost is
the multiplier overlay at about 3 percent of block logic and the adder
at about 10 percent, about 10 percent of DSP cost once the routing
interface is amortized and 0.5 to 5 percent of the 20 nm die. The
half-precision sibling packs two products and their sum into the same
block for reduced-precision workloads; fp32 keeps the full
single-precision product and the single reduction chain per block.

The family's space is opened by no unit template; the variant is documented for the knowledge base alone.
