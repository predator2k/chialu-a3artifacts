# fp16_fp32

The Agilex half-precision mode: the block holds two low-precision
multipliers, a low-precision adder and a subsequent single-precision
adder, accepts four binary16 multiplier inputs, correctly rounds the
two products and their sum, flushes subnormals, and converts the
low-precision sum to single precision before the single-precision
addition, so one block delivers a two-term dot product accumulated in
fp32.

This is the pick when the products are half precision and the
accumulation must stay single precision, since the block does the
format conversion and the two-product sum internally and the fp32 adder
then joins the reduction chain. The mode is Agilex-specific, whereas
the fp32 single-precision multiply-add sibling is available on Arria
10, Stratix 10 and Agilex. The bfloat16 modes are the same structure
with a different low-precision format. The contract is correctly
rounded products and sum with flush-to-zero on inputs and outputs.

The family's space is opened by no unit template; the variant is documented for the knowledge base alone.
