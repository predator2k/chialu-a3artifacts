# bcd8421

Conventional BCD-8421 throughout: partial products in 8421 are
reduced by radix-10 carry-save adders and carry counters, six levels
for sixteen carry-save partial products, and a parallel-prefix
carry-save-to-BCD converter produces the 32-digit product. In a
combined binary/decimal unit each 4-bit column is instead reduced
independently by a binary CSA tree and a lookahead adder, and a
decimal-mode stage converts the column sum into units, tens and
hundreds digits.

It is the pick when the operands, the addend or a shared binary
datapath already use 8421 and a code conversion on every partial
product is not wanted: the column-wise form is easier to share between
binary and decimal than a conventional CSA tree. Its cost is the
decimal carry-save cell: the 16-digit combinational multiplier has
about twice the delay and 1.5 times the area of a radix-4 binary
double-precision multiplier in 90 nm, and each binary column sum needs
a decimal conversion stage. bcd4221 and xs3_odds remove the decimal
cells and let binary compressors do the reduction.
