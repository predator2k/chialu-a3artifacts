# bcd4221

Partial products and carry-save words in the BCD-4221 code, whose
digit weights 4, 2, 2, 1 make all sixteen 4-bit patterns valid
decimal digits, so a slightly modified binary 3:2 CSA tree reduces
decimal rows: the sum digit is valid as it stands and the carry digit,
worth twice its weight, is doubled by a decimal doubler or assimilated
as 2H + S. The 1X to 5X multiples are generated in 4221 and the final
words are converted to BCD-8421 for the decimal carry-propagate adder.

It is the pick for a parallel BCD multiplier that wants a binary
compressor tree and a nonredundant output: the origin design and its
pipelined successor use it with signed-digit radix-10 recoding and a
direct decimal CPA, and the p:2 CSA trees can be organized for area or
for delay. Against bcd8421 it removes the radix-10 carry-save cells
and carry counters; against xs3_odds it still needs the carry-propagate
3X multiple and a doubling step per level, which ODDS replaces by a
constant correction and concurrent carry counting. The radix-5 form
mixes 4221 and 5211 levels in one tree.
