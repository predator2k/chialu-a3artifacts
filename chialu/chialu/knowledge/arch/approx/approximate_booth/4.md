# 4

Radix-4 Booth recoding with an approximate encoder or generator:
the p least-significant partial-product columns use a simplified
encoder table (R4ABE1 with two XOR-2 gates and one AND-2,
R4ABE2 with one XOR-2 gate) or a replaced generator (PPG-2S turns
plus or minus 2A into plus or minus 1A, PPG-1S drives one product per
row from OR-consolidated multiplicand bits), the remaining columns
keep the exact encoder, and an exact tree with a carry-lookahead
adder finishes the product.

Radix-4 is the pick when the error must stay small and be tuned
column by column: a 16-bit multiplier with 12 approximate columns
reaches an NMED of 0.27e-5 at 0.484 pJ PDP in NanGate 45 nm against
the exact radix-4 design, and the rectangular replacement models
keep image PSNR above 80 dB with half the width approximated in
TSMC 65 nm. It costs more partial-product rows than radix-8 and
leaves the reduction tree exact, so the sibling with an exact
encoder and approximate compressors in the low columns wins on MRED
per delay and area; radix-8 is the sibling when the 3Y adder is the
natural place to cut.
